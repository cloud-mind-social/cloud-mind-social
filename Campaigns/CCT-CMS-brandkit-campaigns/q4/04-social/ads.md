# Meta ads — Q4 Laguna Starter Package

**Status:** Draft. Ad spend and launch need Seul's approval. Budget figures are suggestions, not commitments.

## Set-up

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Objective | **Engagement → Messages** (Click-to-Messenger). Conversations matter more than clicks for this offer. |
| Location | Laguna, by city/pin with radius: Calamba +10 km, Santa Rosa +8 km, Biñan, San Pedro, Cabuyao, San Pablo +10 km, Los Baños, Santa Cruz +10 km. Exclude Metro Manila for the first two weeks to keep it local. |
| Age | 22–60 |
| Detailed targeting | Small business owners; Facebook Page admins; interests in entrepreneurship, food business, salon, online selling. Test against Advantage+ audience with location only. |
| Placements | Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook/Instagram Stories, Reels. |
| Language | Ad sets split: English vs Taglish creative. |
| Budget (suggested) | ₱150–₱300/day for 7 days as a test, then shift to the better ad set. |
| Messenger greeting | Use the auto-reply in [messenger-scripts.md](../05-outreach/messenger-scripts.md). |
| Special category | None (not credit, employment, housing, or politics). |

## Rules

- Don't use "guaranteed", "best", "No. 1", or before/after results until real, permissioned proof exists.
- Don't target by personal attributes in the copy ("Are you struggling…?").
- Track inquiries with the keyword START and a "How did you hear about us?" question in Messenger.

## What to watch (week 1)

Cost per messaging conversation started, reply rate, inquiries that complete the intake, and paid starts. Record the baseline before setting targets.
