# Reel / TikTok scripts — Q4 Laguna Starter Package

**Status:** Draft scripts for Seul's review. Any person on camera must be real CMS team (Seul, Elli, Merille or another contributor) with their permission. No actors presented as clients. Show real pages only with the owner's permission; otherwise use a CMS-made demo page labeled "sample page".

Format: 9:16, 20–40 seconds, burned-in subtitles in Inter (Brand Guidelines §10), Fraunces for the opening title only. Keep text away from the bottom 20% and right edge (platform UI).

---

## R1 · "I-scroll natin ang page mo" (Let's scroll your page) · ~30s

| Time | Visual | Voice / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Phone screen, thumb scrolling a **sample** business page | Title: *I-scroll natin ang page mo* |
| 3–10s | Zoom on missing hours, old cover photo, last post from months ago | VO: "Walang oras. Walang presyo. Last post, August pa." |
| 10–20s | Same page, now with hours, pinned post with prices, fresh cover | VO: "Ito yung inaayos muna namin bago gumawa ng bagong posts." |
| 20–28s | Seul (or team member) at a laptop, talking to camera | "Tapos ipapakita namin sa'yo ang plano for the month. Pag okay na sa'yo, kami na gagawa." |
| 28–32s | End card: ₱1,000 · 30 days · one platform · Message START | |

## R2 · "Isang araw ng 10 minutes" (A day of ten minutes) · ~25s

| Time | Visual | Voice / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Timer set to 10:00 | Title: *10 minutes a day sa page mo* |
| 3–15s | Screen recording (sample page): replying to "HM po?", "Available pa?", "Saan location?" | Text: *Sinasagot ang comments* · *Pinapasa sa'yo ang orders* |
| 15–22s | Owner's POV at a busy counter (B-roll, real and permissioned, or illustrative and labeled) | VO: "Busy ka sa counter. Kami na sasagot." |
| 22–25s | End card | ₱1,000 Starter Package · Message START |

## R3 · "Pasko rush checklist" · ~35s

| Time | Visual | Voice / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Christmas lights over a shop front in Laguna (own footage) | Title: *Holiday hours mo, nasa page na ba?* |
| 3–25s | Quick cuts of a sample page being updated | Seul talks over it in her own words while each change appears on screen: holiday hours, gift set prices, how to order. No script; one take, then trim. |
| 25–35s | Seul to camera | "₱1,000, isang buwan, kami na gagawa niyan. Message mo lang kami." |

## R4 · "Behind the calendar" (Instagram-first) · ~20s

Top-down shot of the content calendar being built (sample client), sticky notes or Canva timeline, a hand marking "Approved". Text: *Ganito namin pinaplano ang isang buwan.* End card with price and CTA.

## Shot list to request from Starter clients (for their own reels)

Owner or staff greeting at opening; best seller being prepared; hands packing an order; the shop from outside with signage; a satisfied customer **only with their consent**; before/after of a service. Vertical, natural light, 10–20 seconds each, no music (added in editing).
