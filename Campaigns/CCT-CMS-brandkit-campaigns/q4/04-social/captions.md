# Captions: Q4 Laguna Starter Package

**Status:** Draft copy for Seul's review. Follows the [CMS Content Writing Standard](../../../../CMS-Branding/CMS_Content_Writing_Standard.md). Every Taglish line needs to be read aloud by someone on the CMS team, who should change anything they wouldn't actually say.

**Speaker:** CMS as "we". If Seul wants a caption in her own voice, it has to be built from something that really happened to her, such as a client's comment or something she saw on a local page.

## Voice notes (read before editing)

The first draft sounded like AI. These rules fix that:

- **Say the thing.** Write "We'll run your page for a month," not "Your page, looked after for 30 days."
- **Use details people recognize:** "Last post pa noong August," "PM is the key," "HM po?" Avoid general lines like "what customers notice first."
- **Don't repeat a phrase across assets.** "A plan you approve," "while you run the business" and "the way a new customer would" appeared in nearly every piece. A caption may say each idea once, in its own words.
- **Keep it short.** Most captions should be under 60 words. The graphic already carries the offer.
- **Write Taglish the way people text.** Start from how a Laguna owner would say it, not from a translation of English. Drop "po" where it feels stiff. Write "pa-reserve," not "magpareserba."
- **No neat endings.** Skip the closing aphorism ("Every comment is a customer…") and the rule of three ("design, write, schedule and reply").
- **Don't overuse "honest."** If something is honest, it shows without the label.

Each graphic has its own Facebook caption, plus a separate Instagram or TikTok caption where it runs there. Write each one fresh; don't copy between platforms.

**English**
