# Laguna Facebook groups and community posting

**Status:** Draft for Seul's review.

Local buy-and-sell, town, and small-business groups (Calamba, Santa Rosa, Biñan, San Pablo, Los Baños, UPLB-area and Laguna-wide entrepreneur groups) are where many owners already spend time. They are also easy to annoy.

## Rules

1. Read each group's rules; post promotions only where and when they're allowed (many groups have a promo day).
2. Join as the CMS page where the group allows pages; otherwise a team member's real profile, clearly identifying themselves.
3. Give before you promote. Answer social media questions in comments helpfully, without linking the offer, for at least a week.
4. One promo post per group per allowed period. No copy-pasting the same post into twenty groups on the same day.
5. Never message group members who haven't contacted CMS.

## Helpful post (non-promotional), Taglish

> Tip lang sa mga kapwa negosyante dito sa [Town] bago mag-Pasko: buksan niyo yung page niyo sa phone na parang customer. Nakalagay ba yung holiday hours? May presyo, o "PM is the key" lang? Kung may tanong kayo tungkol sa page niyo, comment lang dito.

## Promo-day post

> Hi po! Cloud Mind Social kami, taga-Laguna. May ₱1,000 kaming package ngayong Pasko: isang buwan, kami na bahala sa Facebook, IG o TikTok ng negosyo niyo. Aayusin namin ang page, gagawa ng posts at reels, at sasagot sa comments. Details sa pic. PM lang sa page namin. Salamat sa admins! 🙏
