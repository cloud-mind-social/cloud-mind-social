# Walk-in, partner and referral outreach

**Status:** Draft for Seul's review. In the Philippine market, personal relationships and recommendations carry a lot of weight (see Track A10 on referral retention and the relationship-first notes in CMS research). This is the highest-trust channel in the campaign.

## Walk-in conversation (60 seconds)

Bring calling cards and a few printed flyers. Go at a quiet hour, never during the lunch or dinner rush. Buy something if it's a shop.

> "Hi po! [Name] po, from Cloud Mind Social, taga-[Town] lang. Nakita ko po yung page niyo, [something specific and true, e.g. "ang ganda ng pics ng cakes niyo"]. May ₱1,000 po kaming package ngayon, isang buwan, kami na bahala sa page. Iiwan ko na lang po itong flyer. Message niyo lang kami kung gusto niyo."

Don't memorize it; say it your way.

Leave the flyer. Do not push for a decision on the spot.

## Partner counters

Ask complementary businesses to display the flyer or calling cards: print shops, co-working spaces, business-permit consultants, bookkeeping offices, local suppliers (packaging, ingredients), bazaar organizers, and Negosyo Center / DTI events where allowed. Offer to return the favor with a shout-out on CMS's page **only if Seul approves**.

## Bazaars and Christmas markets

Q4 has many weekend and Christmas bazaars around Laguna. Use the tarpaulin as a table backdrop, offer a free 5-minute page look-over at the table, and collect contact details with consent (paper form that states the purpose, per the Data Privacy Act).

## Referral card (back of calling card or separate slip)

> **Kilala mo ba ang negosyong kailangan ng tulong sa page nila?** Ibigay mo itong card. [Referral reward, if Seul approves, e.g. a discount on the referrer's next month.]

Any referral reward must be stated plainly and honored.

## Email (for clinics, resorts and professional services)

**Subject:** A 30-day social media start for [Business]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [Name] from Cloud Mind Social, a social media team based in Laguna. I came across [Business]'s page while [honest context, e.g. looking for clinics in Calamba] and noticed [one specific, useful observation].
>
> This season we're offering a one-month package for ₱1,000. We fix up your page, make and schedule a month of posts and reels, and answer comments every day. Details are attached.
>
> If you'd like, I can send a few notes on your page first, no charge, and you can decide after.
>
> [Name] · Cloud Mind Social · hello@cloudmindsocial.com · cloudmindsocial.com

**Needs Seul's explicit approval before use:** the platform plan does not authorize unsolicited messages, and cold email is the same kind of outreach. If approved, send only to business addresses published for inquiries; one email, no sequence.
