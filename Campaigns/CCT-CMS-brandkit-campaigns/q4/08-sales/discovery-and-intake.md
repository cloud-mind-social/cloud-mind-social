## Part A · Discovery questions (10–15 minutes, Messenger call or in person)

Diagnosis first. Listen more than you pitch.

1. Tell me about the business: what you sell, who usually buys, and since when.
2. Where are you located, and where do most customers come from?
3. What are your busiest days and seasons? What's coming up this Christmas season?
4. Who handles the page now? How often does it get updated?
5. Where do customers usually message or find you: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, walk-in, referrals?
6. What would make the next 30 days a success for you? (More inquiries, reservations, orders, or just a page you're not embarrassed by.)
7. Have you worked with a freelancer, agency or boosting before? What happened?
8. Do you have photos or can you take short phone videos?
9. Anything you never want posted? (Prices, staff faces, competitors, sensitive topics.)

**Recommendation step:** if the Starter Package is the right size, say so. If they need ads, a website or multi-platform support, tell them honestly and quote separately. If they don't need CMS yet, say that too.

## Part D · Onboarding checklist (CMS)

- [ ] Day 0: payment confirmed, receipt sent, intake complete, access verified
- [ ] Day 0: baseline screenshots of the page saved (private repo)
- [ ] Day 1–3: social media review delivered
- [ ] Day 1–3: page optimization done; client notified of each change
- [ ] Day 3–5: calendar sent; approval recorded

- [ ] Day 30: wrap-up sent (see below)
