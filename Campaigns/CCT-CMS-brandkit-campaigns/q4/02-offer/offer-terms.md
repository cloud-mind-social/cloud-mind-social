## What's included

| Inclusion | What it means | Proposed quantity / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Social media review | Written review of the page as a first-time customer sees it, with top fixes prioritized | 1 review at the start, delivered by day 3 **[confirm]** |
| Page optimization | Profile photo and cover set-up, bio/about, category, address/map, hours, contact number, action button, pinned post | Using client-supplied logo/photos |
| Content calendar | 30-day plan of topics, formats and dates, approved by the client before production | 1 calendar; 1 revision round |
| Static post creation | Designed single-image posts | **[confirm, e.g. 8]** |
| Basic carousel creation | Multi-slide posts (menus, price lists, steps, before/after) | **[confirm, e.g. 2, up to 5 slides]** |
| Basic reel editing | Editing client-supplied phone clips into short vertical videos: cuts, captions, music from the platform library | **[confirm, e.g. 2, up to 30 seconds]** |
| Caption writing | Caption for every scheduled post, in English, Filipino or Taglish | All posts in the calendar |
| Scheduling | Scheduling all approved content across the 30 days through Meta Business Suite or the platform's own scheduler | 30 days |
| Engagement | Replying to public comments on the page, liking/acknowledging, and flagging inquiries to the owner | 10 minutes per day, **[confirm: 7 days or Mon–Sat]** |

## Not included

Paid ads and ad spend; boosting; photo or video shoots; logo design or rebranding; more than one platform; replying to private messages for sales or orders (we flag them to the owner); website work; guaranteed follower, reach or sales figures. Each can be quoted separately.

## What the client provides

1. Page access as a **role in Meta Business Suite** (Editor or Admin), never a password; TikTok via a shared login only if the owner agrees and changes it after the cycle.
2. Logo, product/service list and prices, hours, location.
3. Photos and raw clips on request (CMS sends a shot list).
4. Approval of the calendar within **[confirm: 2 days]**; unapproved items may be skipped.
5. A contact person who answers questions within a day.

## Timeline (proposed)

| Days | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | Payment, access granted, intake completed |
| 1–3 | Social media review + page optimization |
| 3–5 | Content calendar sent and approved |
| 5–30 | Content produced, scheduled and published; daily engagement |
| 30 | Short wrap-up: what was posted, what the page's insights show, recommendation for next month |

## Proof and permissions

At intake the client chooses whether CMS may use, with their name: before/after screenshots of the page, the page's own insights for the 30 days, and a testimonial in their words. Nothing is published about a client without that written permission. Results are shown with the period, source and limitations (Brand Guidelines §14).

## Privacy

CMS uses page access only for the agreed work, does not download customer lists, and returns or removes access at the end of the cycle if the client doesn't continue. Personal information collected at intake is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and stored in CMS's private clients repository, never in this public repository.
