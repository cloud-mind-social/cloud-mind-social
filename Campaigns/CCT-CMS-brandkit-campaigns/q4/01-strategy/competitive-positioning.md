# Competitive positioning and objection handling (internal)

**Status:** Draft for Seul's review. Internal sales-enablement document; not audience content.

Use this when talking to owners in person, on Messenger or on the phone. Answers are written to be said naturally, in English or Taglish. Keep the CMS stance: diagnose first, recommend the right size, never oversell.

## What makes the Starter Package different locally

1. **It starts with a review, not a posting schedule.** Most cheap offers start posting on day one. CMS looks at the page first and fixes the basics people check (hours, location, prices, message button).
2. **The owner approves the month.** The 30-day calendar is shown before anything goes live.
3. **Scope is in writing**, including what is not included.
4. **It is one platform done properly**, chosen by where the owner's customers already are, instead of thin posting on four.
5. **Language fits the customer.** English, Filipino or Taglish captions.
6. **It is a low-risk first step.** One cycle, ₱1,000, then a clear recommendation based on what happened.

## Objections

**"₱1,000 lang? Baka hindi maayos."** (Only ₱1,000? It might be low quality.)
> Fair question. It's an introductory price so you can see how we work before committing to anything monthly. The scope is written down, you approve the calendar before anything goes live, and you can see every post first.

**"Kaya ko naman 'yan, or yung pamangkin ko."** (I can do it myself, or my nephew can.)
> You can, and some owners should. What we add is the plan and the consistency: a review of the page, a month mapped out around your busy days, and someone answering comments every day. If after the review we think you're fine on your own, we'll tell you.

**"Nag-boost na ako dati, walang nangyari."** (I boosted before and nothing happened.)
> Boosting sends people to your page. If the page doesn't show your hours, prices or how to order, a lot of them leave. That's why we start with the page itself. Ads aren't in this package; if you want them later, we'll quote them separately.

**"Ilang posts ba?"** (How many posts?)
> [Answer with the quantities Seul confirms in offer-terms.md. Until then: "We plan it with you in the calendar, based on what your business needs for the month."]

**"Guaranteed ba na dadami ang customers?"** (Is more customers guaranteed?)
> No one can honestly guarantee that; sales depend on your product, price, location and timing too. What we commit to is the work in the package, on schedule. At day 30 we'll show you what changed on the page and what we'd recommend next.

**"Wala akong time mag-approve."** (I don't have time to approve things.)
> Approval is one look at the calendar, on Messenger, at the start. After that we only need you for photos or clips and quick answers.

**"Wala akong magandang pictures."** (I don't have good photos.)
> Phone photos are fine. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot, like the counter during lunch, your best seller, or your team at work, and we'll design around them. Shoots aren't included, but simple phone content goes a long way.

**"Next month magkano na?"** (How much next month?)
> [State the month-two price Seul sets. Never let a client find out at renewal.]

**"Taga-saan kayo?"** (Where are you from?)
> [Seul's base town in Laguna.] We work online and meet in person when it helps.

## Never say

- "Best / No. 1 / pinakamagaling sa Laguna"
- "Guaranteed sales / followers / viral"
- Any client name, result or testimonial that hasn't been collected with permission
- Anything negative about a named competitor
