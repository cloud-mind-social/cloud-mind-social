## 3. Where this sits in CMS strategy

CMS's locked commercial ICP is Western growth-stage, owner-led businesses; **Philippine growth-stage businesses are the approved secondary geographic market** ([README → Commercial ICP](../../../../README.md)). This campaign is a deliberate, separate PH track:

- It does not change the Western ICP, the October four-platform plan, or its 120 calendar slots.
- Research says to keep Western and Philippine cards and messaging separate ([Phase 2.5.6](../../../../Social-Media-Structure/Phase-2.5/PHASE_2.5.6_Offer_and_Pricing_Architecture.md)). Campaign content should run as **geo-targeted ads, Laguna community groups, print, and in-person outreach**, with only a small number of organic posts on CMS's main pages, so the Western-facing feed stays coherent. **Decision for Seul:** run organically on the main CMS page, or keep it ads/groups/print only.
- It fits CMS's Tier 1 "Fast Food" entry level (convenience, low customization) and feeds the "Presence" tier as the next step.

## 4. Pricing reality check (internal)

From [Pricing-Rate-Research-CMS.md](../../../../CMS-Research/Pricing-Rate-Research-CMS.md) (local PH client rates):

| Provider | Typical monthly range (local clients) |
|---|---|
| PH freelancer | ₱5,000–₱20,000 |
| PH agency | ₱25,000–₱100,000+ |
| CMS Fast Food tier (research recommendation) | ₱1,500–₱5,000 or per task |
| CMS Presence tier (Phase 2.5.6 reference) | ₱15,000–₱30,000 |

₱1,000 is **below the researched Fast Food band** for this scope. That is workable as an introductory offer only if it is treated as acquisition cost. Recommendations for Seul:

1. **Cap capacity**, for example a set number of Starter clients per month, based on real team hours. Say so honestly if slots fill; never invent scarcity.
2. **Make it a first-cycle price.** State in the terms what month two costs (a Presence-level card), so nobody feels surprised. Research shows "transparent pricing, no surprises" is a key trust driver for the Filipino Millennial segment.
3. **Collect proof by design.** Ask for permission at onboarding to use results, a testimonial and before/after screenshots at day 30. This is the real return on the discount.
4. Phase 2.5.6 warns against selling Philippine labor arbitrage as the value. The pitch here is **clarity, a plan the owner approves, and consistency**, never "cheap".

## 5. Audience

Primary: owner-operators of local businesses in Laguna who already have a Facebook page (or Instagram/TikTok) that they update when they have time. Typical categories: food stalls and small restaurants, milk tea and coffee shops, bakeries and home-based food sellers, salons and barbershops, clinics (dental, derma, vet), resorts and private pools (Calamba/Los Baños hot spring area), hardware and auto shops, tutorial centers, boutiques and online sellers.

Mapped to CMS's Filipino personas ([Target Market Research](../../../../CMS-Research/Target-Market-Research-CMS.md)):

| Persona | What they need to hear | Lead message |
|---|---|---|
| **Steady Provider** (Gen X) | Reliability, respect, no risk to the family business | "A plan you approve first. Clear scope, written down." |
| **Uplifting Hustler** (Millennial) | Leverage, time back, growth | "Stop doing the posting yourself at 11pm." |
| **Hopeful Starter** (Gen Z) | Credibility, being taken seriously | "Make the page look like the business you're building." |

Geography: Laguna's six cities (Calamba, Santa Rosa, Biñan, San Pedro, Cabuyao, San Pablo), the capital Santa Cruz, and municipalities such as Los Baños, Bay and Pagsanjan; nearby Batangas and Cavite towns are optional. Ad targeting in [ads.md](../04-social/ads.md).

## 6. Positioning and message house

**Positioning statement (internal):** For Laguna business owners who know their page matters but can't keep up with it, the CMS Starter Package is a 30-day social media cycle that starts with a review of the page and a calendar the owner approves, so the business shows up consistently without a big retainer.

**Umbrella line:** *Ang page mo, kami na ang bahala for 30 days.* (Your page — leave it to us for 30 days.)

| Pillar | What we say | Why it's believable |
|---|---|---|
| Review first | We look at your page like a new customer before we post anything. | The social media review is in the package. Matches CMS's "diagnosis before prescription" value. |
| You approve the plan | You see the 30-day calendar before it goes live. | Content calendar is an inclusion; approval step is in the process. |
| It keeps going while you work | Posts go out on schedule and comments get answers. | Scheduling + 10 min/day engagement. |
| Clear scope | Everything in the ₱1,000 is written down, including what isn't. | Offer sheet + terms. |
| Local | For Laguna businesses, in English, Filipino or Taglish. | Seul's location (confirm) and caption-language capability. |

Locked tagline used as signature: **"We build the bridge from unknown to impact."** The Unknown → Known step of the CMS Dream Ladder is exactly what this package addresses.

## 7. Competitive climate (PH, local)

| Alternative | What owners like | Where CMS's Starter Package answers |
|---|---|---|
| DIY (owner or family member posts) | Free, authentic | Inconsistent; no plan; replies get missed. CMS keeps the owner's voice and adds a plan and schedule. |
| Pamangkin / student helper | Cheap, trusted | Often irregular and leaves when school starts. CMS gives a written scope and a calendar. |
| Freelancer (₱5k–₱20k/mo) | Personal, flexible | Higher monthly commitment; quality varies. CMS is one low-risk cycle to start. |
| Agency (₱25k+/mo) | Capacity, polish | Out of reach for most local shops. CMS is right-sized. |
| Boosting posts only | Fast reach | Reach without a working page wastes money. CMS fixes the page first. |

**Claim rules:** never say "best", "No. 1 in Laguna", "guaranteed results", or quote client numbers CMS doesn't have. No fake reviews, no stock photos presented as CMS clients or staff ([Brand Guidelines §13–14](../../../../CMS-Branding/CMS_Brand_Guidelines.md)). See [competitive-positioning.md](./competitive-positioning.md) for objection handling.

## 10. Proof plan

CMS has no Philippine client proof yet, and every asset avoids implying it does. The campaign builds proof instead:

1. At onboarding, the client signs the proof-permission section of the [intake](../08-sales/discovery-and-intake.md).
2. Day 1 screenshot of the page (baseline); day 30 screenshot plus the platform's own insights for the period.
3. At day 30, ask the client for a short testimonial in their own words. Publish only with written permission and real numbers, labelled with the period and source ([Brand Guidelines §14](../../../../CMS-Branding/CMS_Brand_Guidelines.md) proof card).
