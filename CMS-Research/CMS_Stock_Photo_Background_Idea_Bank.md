**Scope:** 120 original photo concepts for CMS content backgrounds; research-informed art direction and stock-search vocabulary.  
**Status:** Proposed asset concepts; individual images, designs and publication not approved.  
**Audience:** Western owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have outgrown DIY or fragmented social marketing; Western early-stage owners and Philippine audiences retain their separate context.  
**Accountable approver:** Seul / Merille Nacino.  
**Contribution:** AI researched the visual principles and developed the concepts; Seul requested the bank. No stock image has been purchased, downloaded, generated or cleared by this document.

## Direction
Build backgrounds that make an owner recognize a business situation or help explain a CMS decision. Preserve Strategic Social Intelligence, right-sized help and the approved tagline: “We build the bridge from unknown to impact.”

Favor editorial realism: modest work environments, faceless people, useful objects, tactile surfaces, natural light and a clear focal point. These ideas can guide stock searches, original staged photography or later image generation. A search phrase is a discovery aid; it does not establish that a matching asset exists or has usable rights.

Real CMS photographs and genuine, permissioned work artifacts are preferable when demonstrating people, delivery or proof. Stock-style scenes are illustrative backgrounds. They do not represent actual CMS offices, staff, clients, analytics or outcomes.

## CMS visual treatment
| Element | Proposed production treatment |
|---|---|
| Palette | Warm cream #F7F2EA and warm ink #1C1B1A are useful text environments. Echo mist slate #AEB8B4, clay coral #D6553A or deep ochre #C98A2E in restrained props. Photographs need not contain every brand color or perfectly match a hex value. |
| Type | Preserve Fraunces headlines, Inter supporting copy and Space Mono short labels in the design. Do not bake text, logos or labels into the source photo. |
| Light | Window light, soft side light or believable desk-lamp light. Avoid neon blue corporate-tech treatments as the default. |
| People | Hands, over-the-shoulder views, partial silhouettes or backs of people where appropriate. Keep the situation understandable without relying on a facial expression. |
| Environment | Modest service-business studios, home workspaces, consultation areas and everyday work tables; avoid luxury-office imagery that suggests unverified CMS premises. |
| Text space | Start with roughly 40–60% relatively quiet space in text-led backgrounds, then adjust to the actual copy. This is an art-direction heuristic, not an approved quota or platform rule. |
| Transparent text placement | Choose a naturally plain wall, paper surface or dark field behind text. Prefer recomposition or another image when the photograph fights readability. A transparent text box alone does not improve contrast. |
| Screens and documents | Keep generic screens and paperwork blank, obscured or unreadable. Add exact approved interface content separately when needed; use genuine screenshots for claims about real performance. |
| Crop | Prepare square, portrait and vertical compositions separately. Keep the meaningful prop or action inside each intended crop; verify platform interface areas during production. |
| Emotional tone | Gentle humor, recognition, curiosity, relief and persistence. The mood labels describe creative intent, not a proven viewer reaction. |

## Format key
**Static:** single-image feed graphic or a photograph with a connected caption.  
**Carousel cover:** image supporting the opening thought.  
**Carousel interior:** a quiet base behind continuous explanation.  
**Inset:** photograph in a bounded image area next to text.  
**Closing slide:** a calm concluding or next-step composition.  
**Story:** vertical companion with space for the actual interface and stickers.  
**Thumbnail/video title:** a still with a simple focal point and brief text.

Complex human scenes and metaphors usually suit a cover, static or inset better than a text-heavy interior. Quiet tactile bases generally support interior copy better. These are recommendations to test, not fixed template rules.

## How to use a background across a carousel
A photo is a supporting asset; the copy still develops one connected thought.

A practical option is a recognizable scene on the cover, a quieter material or related detail on explanation slides, and a restrained closing image. This is an optional visual pacing approach; it must not become a mandatory writing or slide formula.

Keep related exposures, color treatment and perspective coherent. Avoid reusing a busy photograph behind every paragraph. Where the same photograph is reused, different crops must preserve the intended meaning and readable text area.

Reusing a background asset is different from cross-posting a completed asset. Every platform's finished content still needs its own concept, copy and execution under CMS's no-cross-posting rule.

## Stock-search recipe
Combine **subject + action/environment + composition + light + color**. Start simple, then add constraints rather than searching the full phrase at once.

Example discovery phrases:
- faceless business owner desk warm natural light copy space
- two empty chairs small table cream wall
- wooden puzzle missing piece beige background
- aligned wooden strips gap minimal still life
- hands sorting research cards overhead neutral
- phone printed photos content planning
- ivory linen subtle fold negative space
- charcoal paper matte texture
- cold coffee laptop sticky notes copy space
- small service studio open doorway neutral wall

Useful modifiers: **copy space; negative space; off-center; overhead; flat lay; close-up; faceless; from behind; neutral wall; natural light; warm cream; matte; minimal; editorial photography.**

A composition note such as “blank left half” may work better as a manual selection criterion than as an exact stock search. Search results and stock filters vary by service.

## Brief for later photography or image generation
Use this structure to commission an individual image; it is an internal production brief, not a social caption:

> Photograph [scene] to support [specific CMS content subject]. Use [camera angle] with [prop/action] placed [location]. Reserve [text zone] as a visually quiet area. Natural-looking [light], restrained warm neutral colors, believable materials, editorial realism and a clear focal point. Keep faces out of frame where the concept calls for a faceless scene. ## Selection and verification
Before selecting an individual asset, confirm:
- The scene reinforces the actual subject of the post.
- The reader can understand the object or action at phone size.
- The intended copy zone remains calm after the final crop.
- Contrast passes against the actual photographic background; color hex pairings alone do not prove readability.
- The logo and essential content fit the verified platform layout.
- Source, rights, permissions and illustrative status are recorded.
- Stock or generated scenes cannot be mistaken for actual CMS staff, premises, client work or verified outcomes.
- The exact final design is reviewed separately from this bank.

Avoid as default CMS background choices: staged handshakes; teams cheering at a laptop; fake revenue dashboards; giant growth arrows; piles of money; neon holographic robots; luxury offices presented as CMS's location; and generic smiling groups unrelated to the copy. These can weaken specificity or imply evidence that the image cannot support.
