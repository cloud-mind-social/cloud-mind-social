# CMS 30 Day Daily Content Format Mapping

Prepared: October 1, 2026; Asia/Manila
Status: Proposed concept and format mapping for review. Daily cadence directed by Seul; concepts, source material, writing, design and release decisions still require review.
Basis: [Four Platform Introduction Content Plan](./CMS_Four_Platform_Introduction_Content_Plan.md).
Cadence: one original feed post per platform per day; four daily posts; 30 days; 120 feed posts.
No cross posting. Each concept is developed independently for its platform.

## Calendar interpretation

Day numbers are shared planning slots, not publication dates. Select one common start date before scheduling. Facebook continues after its already-published September 30 introduction; that earlier post is not repeated or counted among these 120 planned slots. LinkedIn, TikTok and Instagram receive their own introductions. This map does not assert that any scheduled concept has already been published.

Stories are optional companion content. They do not replace the four daily feed posts. A Story sequence counts as an additional unit, not one of the 120. A feed poll on LinkedIn can occupy its daily slot; a poll sticker inside a Story remains companion content. If Seul later chooses to count Stories as daily replacements, record that as a separate cadence decision.

## Content classification

| Layer | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Content pillar | The strategic territory | The five approved CMS pillars |
| Publishing format | What is posted | Carousel, Reel, native video, text, single image, poll, Story |
| Strategic content role | What the content helps the viewer understand or decide | The fourteen approved Phase 3.3 roles |
| Content approach | How the idea engages the audience | Handraiser, Relatable, Consumable, Personal Take, Disruptor, Showcase, Authority |
| Trigger or hook | The relevant entry point into a thought | A visible business situation, unresolved question or fair challenge to an assumption |
| Funnel role and CTA | The next appropriate audience decision | Recognition, deeper understanding, method inspection or a relevant conversation |

Trigger is recorded as an angle, not a replacement publishing format or an additional pillar. A trigger can open a diagnostic Reel, narrative carousel or plain text explanation. Avoid manufactured anxiety, shame, controversy and stock correction hooks. The angle does not dictate the public wording.

## Approved strategic role variation

The labels below come from Phase 3.3. They guide internal briefs and do not become public post headings or mandatory slide structures.

| ID | Strategic role | Application and limit |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Strategic Point of View | Show an accountable belief or judgment; original text, image narrative or short video |
| R2 | Diagnostic Breakdown | Examine a symptom within a labeled scenario; Reel, carousel or native video |
| R3 | Educational Explanation | Develop one useful explanation; narrative carousel, text or short video |
| R4 | Decision Framework | Narrate a choice in context; document, photo story or video; structured reference tools remain separate |
| R5 | Checklist or Self-Assessment | One reflective feed question; a full checklist belongs in a separately scoped utility asset |
| R6 | Process Walkthrough | Explain a method through a meaningful example; video, document or narrative carousel |
| R7 | Decision-Trace Case | Trace a sample choice and its basis; accurately label hypothetical or anonymized material |
| R8 | Research Interpretation | Explain a checked finding, population and limitation; document, text or video |
| R9 | Objection and Alternative Response | Clarify a documented buying concern; label uncollected concerns as CMS-selected questions |
| R10 | Proof-of-Method Demonstration | Show a sample brief, annotated artifact or planning view; never imply client results |
| R11 | Verified Case Study | Reserve until actual work, outcomes, context and use-specific permission exist; no placeholder result |
| R12 | Conversation Prompt | Ask a relevant question; text post, native LinkedIn poll or optional Story interaction |
| R13 | Founder or Strategist Reflection | An accountable person reviews the perspective; do not invent experience |
| R14 | FAQ or Clarification | Clarify a genuine question or approved process detail; avoid claiming a question is frequently asked without evidence |

All roles except R11 are available within the daily map. R11 remains reserved for verified and permissioned client evidence; no invented case-study slot is required. The full checklist, procedural framework or functional reference table can live in a separately scoped utility asset. Its feed introduction still develops a natural explanation under the content standard.

## Approach variation

| Approach | Use in this launch |
|---|---|
| Relatable | Make a grounded owner situation recognizable |
| Personal Take | Explain an accountable belief or perspective |
| Disruptor | Investigate a supported assumption fairly; avoid formulaic contrarian claims |
| Showcase | Let viewers inspect a sample or actual work artifact |
| Authority | Explain the reasoning or checked evidence clearly |
| Handraiser | Offer a proportionate next step only when the response route is available |
| Consumable | A brevity consideration for any suitable piece; not a compulsory slot quota |

Approaches are chosen for the source and audience need. They are not a repeating seven-day writing formula.

## Funnel and CTA routing

Days 1 to 7 emphasize identity and recognition; days 8 to 14 deepen understanding; days 15 to 21 make method and accountability visible; days 22 to 30 combine continued trust with selective next-step clarification. These are planning emphases, not assumptions about a viewer's actual stage. A warm viewer can inquire earlier.

| Platform | Default early action | Method or trust action | Selective higher-interest action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Recognize the situation; relevant comment; follow | Consider the explanation or inspect the process | A relevant Messenger conversation or verified website route |
| LinkedIn | Consider the reasoning; relevant discussion; follow | Inspect a document or sample decision | Specific professional conversation or verified website route |
| TikTok | Continue watching; save; follow; genuine question | Inspect the sample reasoning | Profile and available working contact route |
| Instagram | Save; share; explore the profile | Inspect an artifact or method explanation | Relevant DM or verified website route |

An explicit CTA is optional. Never promise an active diagnostic tool, booking mechanism or service route without checking it. A comment, poll vote or DM does not automatically establish qualification.

## Source and production readiness

The map proposes subject directions; it does not claim these source moments have already been collected. Before writing, assign a confirmed accountable reviewer and record a usable source.

| Source situation | Required handling |
|---|---|
| Approved organizational belief | Confirm that the final wording represents the belief and stays within claim limits |
| Personal reflection | The actual speaker confirms the experience or perspective; do not invent a first-person history |
| Fictional scenario or sample | Label it visibly; do not imply actual client work or measured success |
| Actual CMS artifact | Confirm the artifact exists, is accurate and is suitable for public display |
| Research interpretation | Retrieve and verify the particular source before making a finding; preserve population, timeframe and limitation |
| Comment or buyer question | Use a genuine documented question if collected; otherwise clearly label the question as CMS-selected |
| Verified case study | Hold until actual evidence and client use-specific permission are documented |

If a source-dependent piece cannot be substantiated, replace its subject with a source-grounded concept serving the same platform role; preserve one daily feed slot without inventing material.

No cross posting includes captions, finished carousel designs and completed video edits. Brand resources such as the logo, palette and approved typography can be shared. Raw resources may support different executions; the finished content and reasoning must be independently developed.

## Production workload

The 120 feed slots comprise 48 video assets; 36 multi-image/photo/PDF assets; 19 single images with captions; 14 text posts; and 3 LinkedIn polls. Every asset needs its platform-specific caption or accompanying copy. Optional Stories add up to 14 sequences beyond this baseline.

Batch by task: source collection, briefs, writing, design, recording, editing and review. Batch production does not mean duplicating one finished post across platforms. Review day 7, 14, 21 and 30 performance while continuing the daily cadence. Never infer lead targets or revenue outcomes from output volume.
