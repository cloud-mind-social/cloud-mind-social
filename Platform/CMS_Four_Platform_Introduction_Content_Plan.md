## Strategic direction

Each platform introduces Cloud Mind Social independently. A viewer should understand CMS without having seen another platform. Shared identity, audience priority, evidence standards and philosophy connect the channels. Each channel has original subject choices, creative concepts, writing, visuals or video edits and CTAs. No cross posting or adaptations of a completed post between platforms during this launch.

Commercial audience remains Western growth-stage owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have outgrown DIY or fragmented social media. Western early-stage owner-operators are a secondary development audience; Philippine growth-stage businesses are a separate secondary geographic market. These are not equal commercial priorities. A platform does not imply a different ICP or demographic.

The launch should make CMS recognizable for understanding the business before recommending social activity. Services support that story; a service menu does not lead it.

Facebook remains the primary relationship/acquisition environment; LinkedIn receives serious attention because of CMS's Western B2B audience. TikTok is initially a discovery experiment. Instagram presents the method visually while supporting discovery.

## Production rhythm

User-directed rhythm: one original content post per day per platform across 30 days. This is four posts daily and 120 planned platform posts. Facebook's existing September 30 Day 1 is prior context and is not repeated or counted among these 120 new slots. The weekly tables below are starter concept banks; the [daily format mapping](./CMS_30_Day_Daily_Content_Format_Mapping.md) expands the plan into 30 slots per platform. Days 29 and 30 include content as well as review. This cadence is the owner's direction, not a platform-mandated frequency.

Sequence: week 1 introduces relevance and identity; week 2 explains CMS's judgment; week 3 makes its method and accountability visible; week 4 clarifies fit and opens a proportionate next step. Individual viewers may enter at any point.

Stories and polls may appear in the format mapping. The daily baseline is one feed post per platform; companion Stories extend that platform's own material and are optional additions. A Story does not replace the required feed post. Genuine comments and relevant public conversations are source opportunities; the plan does not authorize unsolicited messages.

## Facebook introduction plan

Priority pillar: P3, The Reality of Running a Growing Business.
Editorial direction: Speak to the practical experience of managing a business with social media still sitting on the owner's desk. Show how CMS notices capacity, responsibility and coordination. Maintain Western commercial priority; introduce Philippine examples only with their own context and evidence.
Native formats proposed: multi-image posts, grounded text posts, original Reels and discussion posts. Do not label an organic multi-image post as an advertising carousel.

| Week | Original concept A | Original concept B | Intended movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Retain the approved, published Day 1 CMS introduction and website CTA. Do not repeat an introduction immediately. | P3; a source-grounded reflection about social work being postponed behind urgent business responsibilities. Text plus an authentic working image. | Recognize CMS and a relevant owner situation |
| 2 | P3; a discussion about who makes the final marketing decision when several people contribute. Supporting pillars function as diagnosis, reasoning, method and transparent learning inside a relationship-led environment. Comments may invite recognition; a relevant inquiry can move to Messenger or a verified website contact route. Do not solicit sensitive business detail in public comments or offer a full public diagnosis.

## LinkedIn introduction plan

Priority pillar: P2, Strategic Social Intelligence.
Editorial direction: Introduce CMS through its business judgment. Discuss objectives, coordination, responsibility, measurement and buying decisions in plain language. Professional context does not justify corporate jargon.
Native formats proposed: original perspective posts, narrative PDF documents and short native analysis videos. PDFs develop a coherent explanation rather than repeated slide formulas.
CMS Company Page carries organizational method and perspective. Founder commentary, if separately authorized, adds personal source-grounded thinking; it does not duplicate the Company Page post. Accurate public affiliation is required; no founder-profile changes are assumed here.

| Week | Original concept A | Original concept B | Intended movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P2; introduce CMS through a business planning question: what social media is expected to accomplish as the company grows. Original perspective post. | P3; examine how growth can complicate marketing ownership in a clearly labeled business scenario. Narrative document. | Establish relevant strategic territory |
| 2 | P2; show how the same engagement figure could inform different decisions for two fictional service businesses. Explain the contextual limits. | P1; discuss what a buyer should clarify internally before commissioning social media support; develop the explanation through one situation. | Demonstrate judgment and diagnosis |
| 3 | P2; trace one recommendation from business objective through audience need to a content decision in a labeled sample. | 4 | P2; describe CMS's belief about matching strategy and specialist capability to a defined need; distinguish the model from verified current staffing. | P5; examine one checked research finding, its relevant population, its limitation and the business question CMS would still need to test. | Support credibility and proportionate intent |

Supporting pillars address owner context, buying preparation, practical method and defensible evidence. Relevant public discussion, professional connections and website exploration support movement. No automatic outreach or assumed service activation is included.

## TikTok introduction plan

Priority pillar: P1, Diagnose Before Prescribing.
Editorial direction: Begin with a recognizable question or visible situation and make CMS's diagnostic thinking understandable. Introduce the brand through a useful explanation. Humanization comes from an accountable perspective and real work; face-to-camera appearance is optional.
Native formats proposed: original vertical videos, voiceover with real work footage, annotated fictional page examples, screen recordings and responses to genuine comments. A proposed starting duration of approximately 20 to 45 seconds is a creative test, not a platform rule. Use captions and safe text placement. No long logo opener.
Search subjects are hypotheses until checked. Creator Search Insights, if available to the account, may help identify relevant searches and gaps. Trend use requires an actual fit with the subject.

| Week | Original concept A | Original concept B | Intended movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P1; demonstrate how an unclear offer on a fictional service-business page would shape CMS's first question. Identify CMS briefly within the explanation. | P3; discuss the situation of drafting content after a full day running a business, using an accountable belief or real source rather than an invented personal story. | Recognize a business question and the human perspective |
| 2 | P1; use a fictional page with warm inquiries but slow responses to show why CMS examines the inquiry journey. | P2; interpret one audience signal and the additional context needed before making a recommendation. | Understand diagnosis and signal interpretation |
| 3 | P1; explain what CMS would investigate when an owner wants more followers to address a specific business concern. Clearly label the situation hypothetical. | P4; record a real sample planning workspace and explain one question CMS resolves before production begins. | See CMS's process in action |
| 4 | P1; answer a genuine owner question from comments, if available. Otherwise use a labeled CMS-selected question about clarifying social media's role. | P5; review the launch question the videos were meant to test, what the available analytics indicate and what they cannot establish. | Build early trust; invite relevant continued interest |

Supporting pillars supply owner reality, strategic interpretation, visible work and honest learning. Address owners' decisions rather than tutorials aimed primarily at other marketers. The immediate action is continued viewing, following, saving or a relevant response. A website path is used only if the account's link feature is available and the destination works. Organic distribution into Western target markets is not assumed; review actual audience geography and business relevance.

## Instagram introduction plan

Priority pillar: P4, Clarity in Practice.
Editorial direction: Make CMS's method recognizable through visual storytelling. Every design should reveal a decision or a piece of reasoning. A polished grid alone cannot establish strategic capability.
Native formats proposed: original narrative carousels, Reels showing work and Stories extending the account's own feed concepts. Use approved CMS typography, palette, logo assets and restrained pathways or annotations. Motion should explain the thought. No replacement logo or arbitrary redesign.

| Week | Original concept A | Original concept B | Intended movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P4; introduce CMS through a fictional sample brief becoming a clear content decision. Narrative carousel, with sample status visible. | P3; show a fictional approval bottleneck visually and explain the owner's role in it. | Recognize the brand and operating context |
| 2 | P4; an original Reel shows a messy sample planning board resolving into a defined objective, with voiceover explaining the reasoning. | P1; develop the question of whether an execution request has a clear purpose through one continuous carousel narrative. | Understand the value of diagnosis |
| 3 | P4; a carousel explains what happens to a business question after discovery, using a clearly labeled method demonstration. | P2; visually trace a decision from customer need to message choice without implying tested results. | Assess the thinking behind the visible work |
| 4 | P4; show how a fictional narrow need leads to a bounded recommendation with a clear stopping point. | P5; show a documented CMS artifact, its purpose and what it demonstrates; distinguish method proof from outcome proof. | Establish method credibility and relevant fit |

Supporting pillars supply context, diagnosis, decision logic and evidence. Suggested profile orientation: CMS introduction, how CMS thinks and a method demonstration, using available profile features. Stories or Highlights may group Start Here, Our Thinking and Our Process. Public client proof is added only when approved. Relevant viewers can save, share, reply or visit the profile; a specific need can lead to a DM or verified website route.

## CTAs and measurement

| Platform | Early introduction actions | Higher-interest path | Useful first-month evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Recognize the situation, respond where useful, follow the page | Relevant Messenger conversation or website contact | Substantive owner comments, relevant conversations, website visits and human-assessed inquiry fit |
| LinkedIn | Consider the reasoning, contribute relevant context, follow | Professional conversation or website exploration | Relevant follower/visitor characteristics where available, substantive discussion, target-business inquiries and source-reported website activity |
| TikTok | Continue watching, save, follow, ask a genuine question | Profile visit and an available working contact path | Watch time, completion where available, search discovery, profile visits and audience geography/relevance |
| Instagram | Save a useful explanation, share, explore the profile, reply | Relevant DM or website contact | Saves/shares, profile activity, Story replies and relevant DMs; assess who is responding |

A view is discovery evidence; a comment does not automatically establish intent; an inquiry is not a qualified lead; a sale requires its own commercial records. Compare each channel with its own baseline rather than treating unlike metrics as equivalent.

Days 29 and 30: publish the mapped daily content and also review what viewers understood about CMS, which source-grounded concepts prompted relevant response, whether audience geography and business context match the priorities, whether production is sustainable and whether contact routes work. Retain or revise concepts based on that evidence. No promised numerical lead or revenue targets are set without baselines.

## Human voice and evidence implementation

Each concept requires a source before drafting: approved organizational belief, checked research, authentic work artifact, genuine observation or permissioned conversation. Internal pillar and funnel assignments guide relevance; they do not dictate public sentence or slide patterns.

Audience content follows the approved Grounded human perspective standard. No numbered lessons, disguised lists, repeated rhetorical structures, stock correction hooks, generic motivational claims or invented experience. A carousel divides a coherent thought. Public education remains covered by the standard. A CTA is used only when it follows naturally from the piece.

Seul or another confirmed accountable reviewer verifies the perspective, facts and final wording. Record claims, limitations and permissions internally. Sample work is labeled as sample work; research does not become a client result; hypothetical situations do not become testimonials.
