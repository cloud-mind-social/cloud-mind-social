## Photography intake

Collect an original image, preferred public name and role, permission and intended use; retain an uncropped original. Follow the central photography guidance. Authentic CMS portraits and working-session photographs remain an implementation need unless separately supplied and verified.

## Setup and verification

1. Confirm the correct Cloud Mind Social kit and authorization for native setup.
2. Read central specifications and distinguish approved assets from proposals and explorations.
3. Populate each field from its source; keep source links in the working setup record.
4. Verify packet filenames, rights, asset approval and font availability before upload.
5. Check the actual exported result for readability, contrast, crop and appropriate proof labels.
6. Verify the approved tagline wording; asset typesetting and lockup review remain separate.
7. Record what was configured, by whom, when, and which source revision was used. Do not mark unperformed setup complete.

The proposed reusable-template starting set remains insight, diagnostic comparison, process explanation, specialist profile and proof/report layouts. Develop only after authorization; validate realistic content lengths and follow the writing standard without mandatory repeated slide sequences. Duplicate a master before adapting an individual asset; keep approved assets, explorations and archived variants distinct.
