# CMS Content Writing Standard

**Status:** Approved by Seul; Batch 2 implementation submitted through PR.
**Approval:** 2026-09-22; includes the combined A, B and C rule, Grounded human perspective.

## Authority and scope

This is the authoritative writing standard for audience content produced for Cloud Mind Social and client brands. It preserves approved identity, strategic decisions, evidence requirements, and production gates. Adopting it does not open content production or publication; those require separate authorization.

It applies to social posts, captions, carousel copy, video and Reel scripts, Threads, articles, newsletters, and equivalent audience content. Educational content is fully covered; teaching something does not exempt an asset.

Internal strategy, research, phase documents, brand foundation, governance, briefs, pricing, proposals, reports, diagnostic deliverables, and Agent HQ retain structured formats. Fixed identity assets, bios, functional website copy, functional FAQs, and separately scoped utility assets retain their applicable requirements.

Classification follows purpose. An educational article on a website remains covered. Calling a feed carousel a checklist or FAQ does not make it an exempt utility asset. Strategy determines what a piece is for; this standard governs its expression.

## Core writing rule

> Content must read as a real person sharing an observation, realization, experience, or belief. Do not use numbered points, enumeration patterns, bullet-style thinking, repeated sentence structures, contrast framing, generic motivational language, or predictable copywriting patterns. Avoid phrases such as: Point 1 / Point 2 / Point 3; lists disguised as paragraphs; repeated sentence structures; repeated paragraph structures; “Here's the truth”; “Most people”; “Not this, but this”; or any structure where readers can predict the next sentence. Let thoughts unfold naturally, the way people actually talk, reflect, or tell stories.

Clarity, accuracy, and natural progression remain essential. Artificial sentence variation or withholding necessary information does not improve compliance.

## Grounded human perspective

Content must express a perspective that a real, accountable person has reviewed and stands behind. Its source may be a genuine experience, observation, conversation, question, belief, or documented research. Research findings and interpretations must remain distinguishable from firsthand experience.

Write in language that person would naturally use. Let the explanation develop coherently; avoid repeated rhetorical patterns, disguised lists, stock contrasts, and predetermined endings. Logical progression is welcome; unpredictability is not a goal by itself.

Record the responsible person internally for every piece. Publicly name the speaker when the content relies on their personal experience, quotation, testimony, or individual authority, with their permission. An approved organizational perspective may appear under the brand’s name without a personal byline. Permissioned, anonymized accounts must remain truthful and traceable internally.

Before approval, the responsible person confirms that the perspective represents them, the claims match the evidence, and the wording does not imply experiences or results they cannot substantiate.

A slogan, pillar, funnel stage, or desired audience realization may guide internal relevance checks; it cannot substitute for source material. Research must never be recast as a personal experience, client conversation, or observed business result.

Read the piece aloud. Check whether the speaker would use those words, whether each paragraph advances the thought, and whether converting it into bullets leaves its structure essentially unchanged. Flag repeated openings, interchangeable paragraphs, disguised enumeration, stock reversals, and predetermined endings.

The ending should follow from the thought. A CTA, when strategically justified, must sound like the same speaker; every piece does not need one.

## Truth, evidence, and permissions

AI may organize, edit, and clarify supplied material. It must not invent experiences, clients, quotations, sources, credentials, outcomes, or approvals.

Claims must stay within available evidence. Separate verified facts, research findings, interpretations, assumptions, beliefs, and unknowns. Research approval does not establish live commercial validation.

Record sources sufficiently for reviewers to locate and check them. Preserve relevant dates, populations, limitations, and context. Do not present paraphrases as direct quotations.

Hypotheticals must be clearly identified and must never function as evidence of actual results.

Anonymized stories require permission and a documented source. Remove identifying details without inventing replacements. Hold the story if anonymity would mislead or permission is missing.

The speaker or accountable reviewer must confirm factual accuracy and voice fidelity before release. Permission to collect a story does not automatically grant permission to publish it.

Completed client intakes, private source material, and approval records belong in the authorized private client repository. Public templates remain blank.

## Brand language

Locked brand wording remains valid identity. The approved tagline remains:

> We build the bridge from unknown to impact.

Meaning-preserving paraphrases may express brand ideas in content. Preserve approved positioning and claim limits without implying replacement identity statements.

Slogans, process arrows, narrative pillars, and brand promises must not become compulsory structures for posts. Locked voice descriptors remain unchanged; natural human expression must meet CMS requirements for clarity, empathy, authority, and truthful claims.

## Educational content and format fit

Educational content develops an explanation through a grounded observation, question, interpretation, or experience. It must not default to numbered lessons, repeated slide formulas, disguised lists, or correction-based hooks.

A carousel may divide a continuous thought across slides; slide count must not dictate a repeated rhetorical sequence.

Reflections, grounded perspectives, and responses to genuine questions are suitable when source material supports them.

Checklists, reference tables, procedural instructions, and frameworks may retain structure in separately scoped utility assets. Their promotional posts remain subject to this standard.

Necessary factual distinctions must remain accurate; remove stock contrast framing without distorting the explanation.

## Relationship to existing guidance

The Brand Guidelines’ situation, implication, and next step sequence remains internal planning guidance; it is not a mandatory audience writing sequence. The same distinction applies to Field Guide layout patterns, strategic frameworks, and evidence collection structures.

Use this standard alongside applicable truth, proof, and approval requirements in Phase 3.6 and Phase 3.8, including Gate 4. Batch 2 does not rewrite Phase 3 source documents.

Use the blank [Content Idea Seed](../Templates/Content_Idea_Seed.md) and [Voice and Story Intake](../Templates/Voice_and_Story_Intake.md) when authorized preparation begins.

## Review and release

The combined review asks:

1. **Grounding:** What supports this piece, and are its limitations clear?
2. **Accountability:** Who confirms its truth and perspective; is public attribution needed?
3. **Expression:** Does it develop naturally in that voice without a formula controlling the writing?

Reviewers also confirm correct scope classification, documented sources and permissions, supported claims, clear assumptions and hypotheticals, preserved brand meaning, and factual and voice approval for the actual version reviewed.

Record the outcome as **revise**, **held**, or **approved for the specified stage**. Verify production and publication authorization separately; writing approval alone does not authorize publication.

Revisit this standard when approved review experience exposes ambiguity or the owner changes its scope. Record amendments explicitly. Do not include example posts, sample captions, scripts, or replacement drafts in this standard or its blank templates.
