## Latest decision

Seul approved the recommended Western commercial beachhead and added Western early-stage owner-operators as a secondary development ICP.

- **Primary commercial beachhead:** Western founder or owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have existing customers and revenue but have outgrown DIY or fragmented social media marketing.
- **Secondary development ICP:** Western early-stage owner-operators with a real offer, early traction, or credible business direction who want to build their business and social foundation with strategic clarity.
- **Secondary geographic market:** Philippine growth-stage, owner-led businesses with the same outgrown-DIY / fragmented-marketing condition.
- **Broader social audience:** Early-stage and growth-stage owners who need education about what social media should do for the business.

The early-stage ICP exists for right-sized commercial guidance, relationship continuity, future growth, partnership, and referrals. It is not an unpaid incubation program. Live validation and entry-offer design remain open.
