## Key A8 conclusion

AI is not simply replacing marketing work. It is changing what is scarce and therefore what clients have reason to pay a premium for. Low-complexity execution is becoming easier, faster, more abundant, and more price-sensitive. Higher-value work shifts toward domain expertise, strategic interpretation, workflow design, human judgment, accountability, trust, and business outcomes.

For CMS Audience Strategy, the strongest implications are: “I can do more myself now” becomes a rational objection; CMS should make diagnosis before production visible; human accountability and specialized context need to be explicit; public proof needs to be specific and inspectable; and AI should be an operating capability rather than CMS's core brand differentiator.
