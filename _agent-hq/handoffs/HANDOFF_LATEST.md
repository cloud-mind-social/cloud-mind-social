## Current audience architecture

Phase 2.5.2 remains the current audience direction:

- **Primary commercial beachhead:** Western founder or owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have existing customers and revenue but have outgrown DIY or fragmented social media marketing.
- **Secondary development ICP:** Western early-stage owner-operators with a real offer, early traction, or credible business direction; support must remain commercially bounded while creating future growth, partnership, and referral potential.
- **Secondary geographic market:** Philippine growth-stage, owner-led businesses with the same outgrown-DIY or fragmented-marketing condition.

## Phase 2.5.4 working conclusion

The current CMS territory is promising but not self-explanatory:

- **Strategic Social Intelligence** is distinctive and aligned with the approved foundation, but too abstract to carry the first buyer interaction alone.
- **Diagnosis before prescription** is the clearest distinction when paired with an explicit social media strategy and support category cue.
- **Business before content** becomes commercially meaningful when expressed through a visible consequence, such as fragmented activity consuming owner capacity or failing to support growth.
- **Right-sized support** needs a clear explanation that depth follows the diagnosed constraint, not post volume.
- **Early-stage owner-operators** need a foundation-and-clarity explanation and a bounded commercial entry path.

Use the working paired language and positioning cards in Social-Media-Structure/PHASE_2.5.4_Positioning_Clarity_Validation.md for live testing. Do not treat them as final public copy.
