## Latest decision

Phase 2.5.2 remains the current audience architecture:

- **Primary commercial beachhead:** Western founder or owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have existing customers and revenue but have outgrown DIY or fragmented social media marketing.

## Phase 2.5.3 working conclusion

For the primary beachhead:

> **The most urgent problem is not a shortage of posts. It is a marketing operating system that has become too fragmented, too unclear, or too burdensome to support the business at its current stage.**

For the secondary early-stage ICP:

> **The core opportunity is helping owners establish strategic clarity and a usable foundation before their marketing becomes fragmented and expensive to reorganize.**

The problem and urgency model is supported by completed Track B evidence. It remains a commercial hypothesis until live prospects confirm the recurring root problem, visible consequence, current trigger, and willingness to pay.
