# Cloud Mind Social — Carousel Post Ideas (Phase 1-Aligned)

**Status:** IDEA BANK — not formal Phase 3 Content Architecture
**Purpose:** Develop carousel post concepts that can be used while CMS continues research and before Phase 3 Content Architecture is formally started.
**Strategic foundation:** Phase 1 — Approved Sept. 3, 2026
**Audience foundation:** Phase 2 — Approved/locked
**Market priority:** Western-first
**Primary objective:** Authority
**Desired perception:** “CMS made me realize what my business actually needs.”
**Core idea:** Make the digital world make sense.

---

## Strategic Guardrails

These ideas are built from the approved CMS foundation rather than from generic social-media content prompts.

### Core territory

- Business before content
- Diagnosis before prescription
- Consistency isn't strategy
- The tactic depends on the problem
- Social media is intelligence
- Right-Sized, Not One-Size
- Specialists Over Generalists
- Guidance Before the Sell
- A System, Not a Solo Act

### Content philosophy

> Create content that helps businesses understand something they previously misunderstood.

The carousel should create a perspective shift, not simply deliver a list of tips.

### Funnel role

Most concepts below sit in **Recognition → Perspective → Trust**. A smaller number move toward **Intent**.

### Important launch constraint

These are **ideas**, not finalized Phase 3 content pillars, cadence, or platform strategy. Do not treat this document as approval of Phase 3.

# VI. CAROUSEL STRUCTURE PRINCIPLE

The strongest CMS carousel format should generally follow a perspective-shift sequence:

**1. Familiar problem**

Show the situation the business owner recognizes.

**2. Challenge the assumption**

Explain why the obvious interpretation may be incomplete.

**3. Diagnose the underlying issue**

Separate symptom from cause.

**4. Reframe the problem**

Show the audience what they should actually be thinking about.

**5. Give a useful framework**

Provide questions, a decision tree, comparison, or diagnostic model.

**6. Connect the lesson to business context**

Explain why the distinction matters commercially.

**7. CMS perspective / CTA**

End with the next useful thought, not an aggressive sales pitch.

The intended audience response is:

> **“I hadn't thought about it that way.”**

That response is more strategically valuable to CMS than simply:

> “That was a useful social media tip.”

---

# VII. CONTENT QUALITY FILTER

Before developing any idea into a finished carousel, ask:

1. Does this help the audience understand something they previously misunderstood?
2. Does it start with the business rather than the platform?
3. Does it distinguish symptoms from underlying problems?
4. Does it express an approved CMS point of view?
5. Does it avoid generic “social media tips” territory?
6. Does it avoid making unsupported claims?
7. Does it avoid selling a service before establishing the need?
8. Is the CTA appropriate to the funnel stage?
9. Does the platform adaptation fit the platform's role?
10. Would the audience remember the **idea**, not just the carousel?
