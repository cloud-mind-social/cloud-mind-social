# Phase 3.2 Deliverable 01 — Content Pillar Matrix

**Status:** Working document

| Pillar | Audience realization | Strategic job | Funnel role | CTA direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnose Before Prescribing | More content may not solve the real problem | Establish diagnosis-first thinking | Recognition / realization | Social Signal Check |
| Strategic Social Intelligence | Social signals become useful when connected to business context | Demonstrate judgment | Trust / consideration | Signal-to-Strategy Review |
| Reality of a Growing Business | Fragmentation may be a capacity or coordination problem | Show audience understanding | Recognition / trust | Identify the constraint |
| Clarity in Practice | A clear process reduces uncertainty | Make the CMS method visible | Consideration / trust | Explore the diagnostic pathway |
| Proof, Signals and Commercial Learning | Credibility requires transparent evidence | Build defensible trust | Trust / intent | Review proof or begin a bounded conversation |

## Review questions

- Does each pillar support CMS's diagnosis-before-prescription positioning?
- Does each pillar serve a different audience realization?
- Can the pillar produce educational, analytical and proof-based content without becoming a list of services?
- Is the CTA appropriate for the audience's level of intent?
