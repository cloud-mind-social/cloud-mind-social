## Owner assignment in practice

For each route, the **accountable route owner** accepts responsibility for the entire advertised next step. A **backup** accepts a defined period of coverage, including absence and overload. Supporting contributors may write content, operate the destination, review claims, qualify inquiries, approve offers and record measures, but their work does not replace the accountable owner's handoff. Seul's strategic approval and distinct client-result authorization are decision gates; they are not an assumption that Seul personally monitors every inquiry.

The [Phase 4 draft](./PHASE_4_Funnel_Content_Strategy_Working_Draft.md#what-assigning-owners-means-before-a-route-goes-live) lists the functions and minimum route assignment record. Record the actual names, accepted responsibilities, backup, coverage window, notification test and escalation path before a route is called working and tested. The assigned owner can pause a broken or uncovered route, escalate a claim or commercial issue and record the next action. Do not place client information or access credentials in this public strategy file.

## Closeout judgment

**No unresolved conceptual contradiction blocks final Phase 4 strategy review.** The corrected self-assessment path, proof interpretation and ownership model make the planning matrix coherent. The draft remains owner-approved as a working strategy; its *final strategy closeout* still requires Seul's review and dated decision. Working destinations, named assignments, offer economics, actual proof permissions, measurement baselines, production and publication remain separate activation or release dependencies. A Green planning decision does not certify any of them.
