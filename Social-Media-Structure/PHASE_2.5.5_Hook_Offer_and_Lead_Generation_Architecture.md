# Phase 2.5.5 — Hook Offer and Lead Generation Architecture

**Status:** Approved direction; live testing pending  
**Track:** Brand & Strategy; Commercial Viability & Validation Gate  
**Date:** September 17, 2026

## Purpose

Create a lead-generation ladder that moves qualified buyers from recognition to conversation to an appropriate paid engagement.

The system must support CMS's approved positioning:

- Strategic Social Intelligence
- Diagnosis before prescription
- Business before content
- Right-sized help
- Social media as discovery
- Website as the owned conversion environment

This phase defines the hook architecture. It does not finalize pricing, service packages, acquisition spend, or public positioning claims.

## Core decision

CMS should use one diagnostic system expressed at different commitment levels, not a catalogue of unrelated lead magnets.

The recommended ladder is:

1. **Social Signal Check**; ToFu recognition.
2. **Signal-to-Strategy Review**; MoFu conversation.
3. **Social Clarity Intensive**; BoFu paid diagnosis.
4. **Right-sized strategy or execution engagement**; only after diagnosis.

A hot lead may enter directly at the paid diagnostic stage. Buyers should not be forced through every funnel stage.

## Audience priority

### Primary commercial beachhead

Western founder or owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses with existing customers and revenue that have outgrown DIY or fragmented social media marketing.

### Secondary development ICP

Western early-stage owner-operators with a real offer, early traction, or credible business direction. This path must remain commercially bounded; it is not unpaid incubation.

### Geographic sequencing

Western first; Philippines second. The Philippine market should not replace the first Western validation test.

## ToFu; Social Signal Check

**Audience temperature:** Cold or problem-aware  
**Objective:** Create recognition and curiosity.

The Social Signal Check is a short self-assessment that helps an owner determine whether the visible problem is content, capacity, direction, coordination, measurement, or foundation.

Suggested areas:

- What social media is supposed to accomplish
- Who owns decisions and approvals
- Whether providers work from one direction
- Whether activity connects to business outcomes
- What currently consumes the owner's time
- What the business has already tried
- What must change in the next three to six months

The result should identify a problem category, not provide a complete strategy.

Possible calls to action:

- Reply SIGNAL to receive the check.
- Comment CLEAR if social activity feels busy but disconnected.
- Start with the question most agencies skip: what does social media need to do for the business?

## MoFu; Signal-to-Strategy Review

**Audience temperature:** Warm and actively exploring  
**Objective:** Turn curiosity into a qualified conversation.

This is a short, bounded review for people who completed the check, engaged with several CMS pieces of content, replied with a specific problem, arrived through a referral, or show an active trigger.

The review identifies:

- The stated problem
- The likely underlying constraint
- The visible business consequence
- The next decision the buyer should make
- What CMS would not recommend yet

The review should return a limited number of observations and one priority. It should not become a free strategy engagement.

Possible call to action:

> Apply for a Signal-to-Strategy Review if your social activity is creating more work than clarity.

This may begin as a limited pilot for qualified prospects. It can later become a low-cost or creditable service after live testing.

## BoFu; Social Clarity Intensive

**Audience temperature:** Hot and urgency-driven  
**Objective:** Convert intent into a paid first engagement.

The Social Clarity Intensive is a finite a-la-carte diagnostic service. It is the preferred entry point for a buyer who is ready to invest but does not yet know the correct depth of support.

Potential scope:

- Business objective and current stage
- Audience and offer context
- Existing social setup
- Current providers, tools, and internal responsibilities
- Fragmentation and capacity issues
- Role social media should play
- Recommended level of support
- Initial priorities
- Measurement direction
- Next-step options

The deliverable should be a concise decision brief, not an oversized audit.

Working promise:

> Leave with a clear decision about what social media needs next, what it does not need, and what level of support makes sense.

The diagnostic may lead to a Presence, Momentum, Authority, or Growth Partner engagement, but the tier should be prescribed only after the problem is understood.

## Early-stage expression

The same system can serve the secondary development ICP with different entry language.

### ToFu

**Social Foundation Check**

> Are you building a social foundation, or accumulating random activity?

### MoFu

**Foundation Fit Review**

Focus on:

- Offer clarity
- Audience direction
- The role social media should play
- What should be built first
- What the owner should not outsource yet

### BoFu

**Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint**

A finite engagement may cover initial business and audience direction, social role, channel priorities, messaging structure, minimum viable content direction, and simple measurement logic.

Qualification should require a real offer, early traction, or credible business direction. The service must not become open-ended coaching.

## Hook content by funnel stage

### ToFu

- More content may be hiding the real problem.
- If social media is active but the business is still unclear, check these five signals.
- Before hiring a social media manager, answer these three business questions.
- Your consistency problem may actually be an ownership problem.
- Not every social media problem needs more posting.

### MoFu

- The issue may not be inconsistent posting; it may be unclear decision-making.
- If three people touch marketing and nobody owns the direction, you have a system problem.
- Your bottleneck may be strategy, capacity, coordination, or measurement.
- You may not need a bigger content calendar; you may need a clearer social role.

### BoFu

- If you are ready to stop guessing, start with a diagnosis.
- Bring your current social setup; leave with a decision about what needs to happen next.
- Your business has changed; your social media operating model may need to change with it.
- Before investing in more execution, confirm what the business actually needs.

These are working hooks for testing, not final approved public copy.

## Channel activation

| Channel | Hook role |
|---|---|
| Facebook | Primary acquisition; problem-recognition posts, polls, Messenger keyword flows, lead forms |
| Instagram | Carousels and Reels that expose hidden problems; DM keyword CTA |
| TikTok | Fast problem recognition; wrong-fix and myth-based videos |
| YouTube | Diagnostic explanations and case-style breakdowns; drive to the Social Signal Check |
| LinkedIn | Business consequences, owner capacity, and marketing operating model |
| Threads | Opinion-led conversation; invite owners to describe what feels fragmented |

The website captures context, qualifies the lead, and routes the buyer to the appropriate next step.

## Lead temperature rules

**Cold:** Viewed, liked, followed, or lightly engaged.

**Warm:** Completed the check, replied with a specific situation, asked a relevant question, or accepted a review.

**Hot:** Has a current trigger, describes a business consequence, asks about scope or timing, shares existing materials, or requests a proposal.

A download or positive reaction alone does not establish buying readiness.

## Measurement

Track movement through the ladder:

- ToFu; assessment starts, completion, replies, qualified clicks
- MoFu; application quality, show rate, recurring problem patterns, trigger strength
- BoFu; diagnostic acceptance, scope fit, conversion to follow-on work, delivery effort, and retention signals

Reach and follower growth are supporting indicators, not the primary success measure.

## À La Carte clarification

The approved working À La Carte menu may include finite, clearly scoped services such as:

- Reels, carousels, static posts, story sets, infographics, captions, repurposing, and thumbnails.
- Profile or bio optimization, social profile visual refresh, limited branding direction, and template starter sets.
- Scheduling-only, a finite Social Operations Pilot, limited community management, analytics reports, and defined content calendars.
- Social Clarity Intensive and other bounded diagnostic or strategic micro-projects.

Full social media management, full brand identity, ongoing strategy, continuous optimization, and strategic growth leadership remain under Presence, Momentum, Authority, and Growth Partner.

This menu is an approved working direction, not a final price list. Service inclusion, scope, packaging, and pricing may be revised when Phase 2.5.6 À La Carte pricing decisions are finalized.

## Guardrails

- Do not make a generic free social media audit CMS's main hook.
- Do not promise followers, reach, leads, revenue, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not sell posting volume as the product.
- Do not give away a complete strategy before qualification.
- Do not force hot leads through a slow nurture sequence.
- Do not let the early-stage path become unpaid incubation.
- Keep pricing and final service scope subject to Phase 2.5.6 and live validation.
