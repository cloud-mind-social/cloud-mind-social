# Phase 3.8 — Content Architecture Operationalization and Production Handoff

**Status:** In Progress  
**Phase:** 3 — Content Architecture  
**Dependency:** Phase 3.7 Content Architecture Integration and Completion Review

## Purpose

Convert the completed Phase 3 content architecture into a practical operating handoff that CMS can use when developing content, reviewing drafts and preparing for Phase 4.

Phase 3.8 does not create posts, a calendar, platform strategies or a campaign. It defines how the architecture will be used consistently.

## Operational content chain

Every CMS content item must pass through this chain:

**Evidence → Audience → Pillar → Theme → Angle → Realization → Funnel stage → Format → CTA → Destination → Measurement → Approval**

Skipping a link creates one of four risks:

- Content that is interesting but commercially irrelevant
- Content that makes claims CMS cannot support
- Content that asks for the wrong next action
- Content that cannot be measured or improved

## Minimum content brief

No content item should enter production without the following fields:

| Field | Required decision |
|---|---|
| Working title | What the idea is about |
| Audience segment | Primary Western, Western early-stage or Philippine secondary |
| Audience problem | What tension or need is being addressed |
| Primary pillar | One Phase 3.2 pillar |
| Theme | One recurring territory |
| Angle | The specific point of view |
| Audience realization | What the audience should understand |
| Funnel stage | Recognition, realization, trust, intent, diagnostic, recommendation or continuation |
| Format | Strategic delivery structure |
| CTA | The next action |
| Destination | Where the audience goes |
| Evidence class | Approved decision, direct evidence, market evidence, interpretation, hypothesis or excluded |
| Proof type | Method, capability, client proof or market proof |
| Claim limitation | What the content must not imply |
| Measurement | What useful movement looks like |
| Risk level | Low, medium or high |
| Reviewer | Who approves the item |

## Production statuses

Use these statuses to prevent unfinished or unapproved content from being treated as ready:

1. **Idea**; rough concept only
2. **Strategically routed**; pillar, theme and funnel role assigned
3. **Evidence checked**; claim and source reviewed
4. **Briefed**; minimum content brief completed
5. **Drafting**; copy or asset is being developed
6. **Internal review**; strategic and evidence review underway
7. **Revision required**; specific corrections needed
8. **Approved for adaptation**; strategic content is approved, platform adaptation may begin later
9. **Platform-ready**; adapted according to Phase 5 rules
10. **Scheduled or published**; release state recorded
11. **Learning recorded**; response and evidence added to the feedback loop
12. **Archived or revised**; content is retired, corrected or repurposed

## Approval gates

### Gate 1 — Strategic routing

Confirm:

- The idea serves one pillar.
- The theme is relevant.
- The audience segment is clear.
- The content supports CMS's strategic position.
- The idea is not merely a trend or posting quota filler.

### Gate 2 — Evidence and claim safety

Confirm:

- The evidence class is recorded.
- The wording matches the confidence level.
- Proof type is represented honestly.
- Sources, permissions and attribution are present where required.
- No unsupported result, guarantee or superlative is included.

### Gate 3 — Funnel and CTA fit

Confirm:

- The audience state is identified.
- The funnel role is appropriate.
- The CTA matches readiness.
- The destination exists or is explicitly marked pending.
- The content does not skip diagnosis when diagnosis is required.

### Gate 4 — Production quality

Confirm:

- The chosen format serves the idea.
- The content is understandable without unnecessary jargon.
- The tone matches CMS.
- The asset does not overpromise.
- The platform adaptation can occur later without changing the strategic claim.

### Gate 5 — Learning capture

After publication or delivery, record:

- Audience response quality
- Useful language
- Objections
- Questions
- CTA movement
- Misunderstandings
- Commercial signals
- Evidence that should update the assumption register

## Content ownership

| Function | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Strategy owner | Protects positioning, pillar logic, audience relevance and evidence boundaries |
| Content planner | Routes ideas through the content brief and funnel architecture |
| Writer or creator | Develops the approved idea without changing the strategic claim |
| Reviewer | Checks clarity, evidence, CTA and governance |
| Platform owner | Adapts approved content for platform context in Phase 5 |
| Commercial owner | Confirms offer, price, scope and destination accuracy |
| Learning owner | Records performance, language and buyer signals |

One person may hold several roles, but the responsibilities must remain distinct.

## Repurposing rules

Repurposing is allowed when:

- The strategic claim remains accurate.
- The audience realization remains intact.
- The format changes because the delivery context changes.
- The CTA remains appropriate to the new audience state.
- The evidence label remains visible internally.
- Platform adaptation does not add unsupported claims.

Repurposing is not allowed when it:

- Turns a hypothesis into a proven claim.
- Turns a method demonstration into a client result.
- Removes important limitations.
- Reuses a high-intent CTA in a low-awareness context without adjustment.
- Copies the same content across platforms without adapting the strategic role.

## Content review cadence

### Before production

Review:

- Pillar and theme fit
- Audience and funnel state
- Evidence class
- CTA and destination
- Risk level

### During production

Review:

- Accuracy
- Tone
- Clarity
- Claim strength
- Format suitability
- Scope and offer language

### After publication

Review:

- Quality of response
- Audience language
- CTA behavior
- Misinterpretation
- Qualified movement
- New objections or questions
- Whether the assumption register needs updating

## Operational metrics

Phase 3 content operations should track:

- Percentage of content with complete briefs
- Percentage approved without major revision
- Percentage with documented evidence class
- Percentage with a defined CTA and destination
- Content by pillar and funnel stage
- Repeated themes and content gaps
- Qualified responses
- Diagnostic actions
- Misrouted or unqualified actions
- Corrections caused by unclear or unsupported claims
- Evidence or assumptions updated from content learning

Do not optimize solely for production volume.

## Readiness for Phase 4

CMS is operationally ready for Phase 4 when:

- The minimum content brief is usable.
- The approval gates can be applied consistently.
- CTA destinations are defined or staged.
- Proof and claim rules are understood.
- Content learning has an owner.
- The Phase 2.5 commercial guardrails are accessible.
- The team can distinguish strategy, production, platform adaptation and funnel execution.

## Readiness for Phase 5

CMS is ready for platform adaptation when:

- Strategic content roles are approved.
- Content formats are stable.
- CTA and destination rules are stable.
- Platform roles are defined separately.
- Adaptation will not change the strategic claim.
- The platform owner understands evidence and governance requirements.

## Handoff package

The Phase 3 handoff package should contain:

- Phase 3.1 evidence and assumption framework
- Phase 3.2 pillar and theme architecture
- Phase 3.3 format and role architecture
- Phase 3.4 content-to-funnel mapping
- Phase 3.5 CTA framework
- Phase 3.6 proof and governance requirements
- Phase 3.7 integration and completion review
- This Phase 3.8 operational handoff
- Approved Phase 2.5 references
- Content brief template
- Claim register template
- Feedback and learning process

## Handoff exclusions

The Phase 3 handoff does not include:

- A finished content calendar
- Platform-specific posting schedules
- Final captions or scripts
- Unapproved public pricing
- Unverified client proof
- Platform-specific growth promises
- Automatic audience expansion
- Work inside www/ or webmail/

## Phase 3.8 completion criteria

Phase 3.8 is complete when:

- The architecture can be used by another collaborator without oral explanation.
- Content briefs capture strategic, commercial and evidence decisions.
- Approval gates are clear.
- Ownership is assigned.
- Learning can flow back into the assumption register.
- Repurposing rules protect the original strategic meaning.
- Phase 4 and Phase 5 boundaries are explicit.
- The complete Phase 3 handoff package is identified.
