# Phase 3.4 — Offer Architecture and Content-to-Funnel Mapping

**Status:** Working draft; offer figures and final scope remain subject to validation  
**Phase:** 3 — Content Architecture  
**Dependency:** Phase 2.5.5 Hook, Offer and Lead Generation Architecture; Phase 2.5.6 Offer and Pricing Architecture; Phase 3.2 Brand Foundation Finalization and Content Alignment; Phase 3.3 CMS Methodology, Content Formats and Strategic Roles

## Purpose

Define how CMS offers are structured and how content supports movement from recognition to a diagnostic conversation and, after diagnosis, to right-sized support.

This document combines the approved Phase 2.5 offer architecture with the Phase 3 content-to-funnel system. It clarifies which commercial route is appropriate for each audience state without turning content into a pressure-based sales sequence.

This document does not define platform-specific publishing, cadence, campaign budgets, final public pricing, contract language, or final sales automation.

## Offer architecture baseline

Phase 3.4 carries forward the commercial structure developed in Phase 2.5.6. It translates that structure into content, funnel, qualification and conversion logic; it does not replace the Phase 2.5.6 working draft or treat its directional figures as final public pricing.

### Commercial design principles

CMS offer architecture is governed by:

* Diagnosis before prescription; understand the business problem before assigning a service.
* Business before content; content is an execution layer, not the product by itself.
* Right-sized help; match service depth to the diagnosed problem, stage and capacity.
* Finite entry services; À La Carte allows a defined test or micro-project without creating an undefined retainer.
* Clear responsibility levels; use Execution → Management → Strategy → Partnership as the commercial depth logic.
* Western first; Philippines second; maintain separate commercial reference cards.
* No generic posting-volume packages, unsupported outcome claims, guaranteed virality or vanity-metric guarantees.
* No unpaid incubation; early-stage support must have a defined scope, boundary and commercial path.

### CMS offer ladder

| Offer layer | Working offer | Commercial role | Routing rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free recognition asset | Social Signal Check | Help a prospect identify a visible social or marketing constraint | Appropriate for recognition and early realization |
| Bounded conversation | Signal-to-Strategy Review | Review the stated problem, likely constraint, visible consequence and next decision | Appropriate for qualified interest |
| Paid diagnostic | Social Clarity Intensive | Produce a finite diagnosis and concise decision brief | Appropriate when the buyer is ready for paid clarity |
| Finite project or execution | À La Carte Services | Solve a specific problem with a defined deliverable, scope and stop point | Appropriate when the need is already clear |
| Core engagement | Presence → Momentum → Authority → Growth Partner | Match ongoing responsibility to the depth of the diagnosed need | Recommended only after diagnosis supports the level |
| Early-stage development | Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint | Establish a bounded strategic foundation for a qualified early-stage owner-operator | Requires a real offer, credible direction and defined need |

A prospect may enter at the most appropriate stage. The ladder is a routing system, not a mandatory sequence.

### À La Carte boundaries

À La Carte follows this structure:

> Specific problem → specific deliverable → specific outcome → finite engagement.

Potential categories include:

* Content production; Reels, carousels, infographics, static posts, captions, repurposing, story sets and thumbnails.
* Profile and limited brand support; profile optimization, social visual refresh, limited branding direction and template starter sets.
* Operations and reporting; scheduling-only, a 30-day Social Operations Pilot, limited community management, analytics reports and defined content calendars.
* Bounded diagnosis and strategy; Social Clarity Intensive, Signal-to-Strategy Review, content pillar strategy, audience segmentation, website or social integration audit and campaign direction.

À La Carte services are not automatically inferior to a core engagement. They are finite by design. Full social media management, full brand identity, ongoing strategy, continuous optimization and growth leadership remain under the core engagement structure.

### Offer fit and scope boundaries

| Offer | Appropriate when | Core output | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social Signal Check | The prospect notices a problem but has not yet identified the cause | Self-identification and a clearer question | A complete audit or guaranteed diagnosis |
| Signal-to-Strategy Review | The prospect can describe a specific situation and needs interpretation | Bounded review and next-decision guidance | Unlimited consulting or a full strategy project |
| Social Clarity Intensive | The prospect is ready to pay for structured diagnosis | Finite diagnosis and decision brief | Automatic eligibility for a retainer |
| À La Carte Services | The problem and deliverable are already clear | Defined asset, batch, project or operational support | Unlimited revisions, hidden strategy or ongoing management |
| Presence | The client needs a credible, organized presence with limited responsibility | Defined presence and right-sized execution | Full growth leadership |
| Momentum | The client needs consistency, coordination and reduced owner burden | Ongoing management, execution and reporting | Full strategic partnership by default |
| Authority | The client needs positioning, strategy, measurement and optimization | Strategy connected to business goals | A posting-volume package |
| Growth Partner | The client needs external strategic leadership and specialist coordination | Ongoing partnership and growth systems | A launch-stage default or guaranteed growth |
| Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint | The owner has a real offer, early traction or credible direction and a defined foundation need | Bounded foundation, channel and measurement direction | Open-ended coaching or unpaid incubation |

### Commercial delivery journey

The operating journey is:

1. Needs assessment
2. Diagnosis
3. Recommendation
4. Proposal
5. Contract
6. Payment
7. Delivery
8. Measurement
9. Proof
10. Renewal, expansion or referral

Each transition must make the next decision, scope, responsibility and evidence requirement visible.

| Responsibility area | CMS responsibility | Client responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Context | Ask the right business, audience and marketing questions | Provide accurate business context |
| Diagnosis | Interpret symptoms, constraints, risks and priorities | Share access, facts, goals and limitations |
| Recommendation | Recommend only the depth supported by the diagnosis | Review fit, scope and tradeoffs |
| Proposal and contract | Define deliverables, boundaries, timeline and payment terms | Approve scope and meet contract requirements |
| Delivery | Perform the agreed work and communicate limits | Provide assets, approvals and timely decisions |
| Measurement | Report agreed indicators and evidence boundaries | Provide business context and outcome feedback |
| Proof and expansion | Document verified learning and recommend the next decision only when supported | Approve use of proof and decide whether to continue, expand or refer |

### Qualification, disqualification and routing

| Buyer signal | Recommended route |
|---|---|
| Wants one specific asset with clear inputs | À La Carte content production |
| Wants content uploaded or organized | Scheduling-only or Social Operations Pilot |
| Has a repeated activity problem but unclear cause | Signal-to-Strategy Review |
| Has an active trigger and needs a decision | Social Clarity Intensive |
| Needs ongoing consistency and reduced owner burden | Presence or Momentum |
| Needs positioning, strategy, measurement and optimization | Authority |
| Needs external marketing leadership and specialist coordination | Growth Partner |
| Early-stage with a real offer and defined foundation need | Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint |
| Wants unlimited coaching, undefined support or guaranteed virality | Re-scope or decline |

CMS should not route every prospect to a retainer. It should not accept work that depends on unsupported promises, unlimited access, post-count reporting alone, or a level of demand the business cannot support.

## Offer-to-funnel mapping

The commercial route should follow the audience's readiness and the evidence available at that point.

| Funnel stage | Audience state | Primary offer route | Content responsibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Notices a symptom but does not understand the cause | Social Signal Check | Name the visible problem without overselling a solution |
| Realization | Suspects the visible problem has a deeper constraint | Social Signal Check or Signal-to-Strategy Review | Help the audience interpret the situation |
| Trust | Evaluates CMS's method, judgment and credibility | Signal-to-Strategy Review or relevant method resource | Show process, boundaries and evidence |
| Intent | Considers outside help and needs a proportionate next step | Signal-to-Strategy Review or Social Clarity Intensive | Clarify fit, risk, scope and diagnostic value |
| Diagnostic conversation | Is ready to share context and be assessed | Social Clarity Intensive or qualified consultation | Prepare the prospect for the diagnostic process |
| Recommendation | Has received a diagnosis and needs a right-sized solution | À La Carte, Presence, Momentum, Authority or Growth Partner | Explain why the recommended scope follows from the problem |
| Delivery | Has selected and approved support | Selected service or engagement | Set expectations, responsibilities and measurement |
| Proof | Has completed a meaningful work stage | Verified proof, learning or case material | Document what was actually learned or changed |
| Renewal, expansion or referral | Is evaluating the next business constraint | Continue, expand, refer or conclude appropriately | Make the next decision visible without manufactured urgency |

Content may route a person toward an offer, but only diagnosis and qualification determine whether that offer is appropriate.

## CMS funnel logic

CMS content supports this progression:

**Recognition → Realization → Trust → Intent → Diagnostic conversation → Recommendation → Right-sized support → Renewal or expansion**

The purpose of the funnel is not to force every person toward a retainer. It is to help the audience understand the problem, reduce uncertainty, choose an appropriate next step and receive support proportionate to the diagnosed need.

## Funnel stages

### 1. Recognition

**Audience state:** The person notices a social-media or marketing problem but may not understand its cause.

**Audience needs:**

- Language for what feels wrong
- Recognition of visible symptoms
- Permission to question the current approach
- A reason to pay attention to CMS

**Primary content pillars:**

- Diagnose Before Prescribing
- The Reality of Running a Growing Business

**Strongest formats:**

- Strategic Point of View
- Diagnostic Breakdown
- Educational Explanation
- Conversation Prompt

**Content job:**

- Name the visible problem.
- Challenge the automatic “we need more content” response.
- Show that the issue may involve capacity, ownership, coordination or clarity.

**CTA options:**

- Save or follow.
- Respond with the situation.
- Complete the Social Signal Check.
- Read a related explanation.

**Do not ask for:** A consultation or retainer before the audience understands the problem.

### 2. Realization

**Audience state:** The person suspects the visible problem may have a deeper cause.

**Audience needs:**

- A way to distinguish symptom from constraint
- A framework for interpreting the situation
- Help comparing possible solutions
- Clarity about what should be examined next

**Primary content pillars:**

- Diagnose Before Prescribing
- Strategic Social Intelligence
- The Reality of Running a Growing Business

**Strongest formats:**

- Diagnostic Breakdown
- Decision Framework
- Checklist or Self-Assessment
- Research Interpretation

**Content job:**

- Reframe the stated problem.
- Explain why different causes require different responses.
- Help the audience avoid buying the wrong type of support.

**CTA options:**

- Apply the framework.
- Complete the Social Signal Check.
- Describe the current constraint.
- Request a Signal-to-Strategy Review when the issue is specific.

### 3. Trust

**Audience state:** The person understands the problem and is evaluating whether CMS is credible.

**Audience needs:**

- Evidence that CMS understands the business context
- Visibility into the process
- Proof that recommendations are reasoned
- Transparency about limitations, scope and responsibilities
- A clear distinction between CMS and a generic provider

**Primary content pillars:**

- Clarity in Practice
- Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning
- Strategic Social Intelligence

**Strongest formats:**

- Process Walkthrough
- Proof-of-Method Demonstration
- Decision-Trace Case
- Research Interpretation
- Founder or Strategist Reflection
- FAQ or Clarification

**Content job:**

- Show how CMS thinks and works.
- Demonstrate process without fabricating outcomes.
- Make uncertainty and evidence boundaries visible.
- Reduce perceived risk.

**CTA options:**

- Review the diagnostic method.
- Inspect a proof-of-method example.
- Read a relevant case study when verified.
- Ask a specific question.
- Explore the appropriate entry offer.

### 4. Intent

**Audience state:** The person believes the problem may require outside help and is considering action.

**Audience needs:**

- Confidence that CMS is relevant
- Clarity about the safest next step
- Understanding of scope and investment
- Answers to objections
- A proportionate offer rather than an oversized commitment

**Primary content pillars:**

- Strategic Social Intelligence
- Clarity in Practice
- Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning

**Strongest formats:**

- Decision Framework
- Objection and Alternative Response
- FAQ or Clarification
- Decision-Trace Case
- Process Walkthrough
- Verified Case Study where available

**Content job:**

- Help the buyer decide whether CMS is appropriate.
- Explain why diagnosis comes before ongoing execution.
- Clarify which entry point fits the situation.
- Address risk without pressure.

**CTA options:**

- Request a Signal-to-Strategy Review.
- Book the Social Clarity Intensive.
- Ask about scope or fit.
- Review the relevant À La Carte pathway.

**Do not ask for:** A large ongoing commitment when the buyer has not yet been diagnosed or qualified.

### 5. Diagnostic conversation

**Audience state:** The person is ready to discuss the situation in context.

**Audience needs:**

- A clear process
- An expectation of what will be discussed
- Confidence that the conversation is not a pressure-based sales call
- A defined decision or output

**Primary content pillars:**

- Clarity in Practice
- Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning

**Strongest formats:**

- Process Walkthrough
- Checklist or Self-Assessment
- Proof-of-Method Demonstration
- FAQ or Clarification
- Decision-Trace Case

**Content job:**

- Prepare the prospect to share relevant context.
- Explain what CMS needs to understand.
- Make the diagnostic output and boundaries clear.
- Set expectations about what CMS may recommend or decline.

**CTA options:**

- Request the appropriate diagnostic.
- Submit the qualification information.
- Book a consultation where appropriate.

**Required destination:** Website, consultation form, qualification flow or approved diagnostic entry point.

### 6. Recommendation and right-sized support

**Audience state:** CMS has enough context to recommend a next step.

**Audience needs:**

- A clear diagnosis
- Explanation of the recommendation
- Scope and responsibilities
- Timeline and decision boundaries
- Confidence that the recommendation is not oversized

**Primary content pillars:**

- Clarity in Practice
- Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning

**Strongest formats:**

- Decision-Trace Case
- Process Walkthrough
- Verified Case Study
- FAQ or Clarification

**Content job:**

- Support the recommendation after diagnosis.
- Show how the recommended scope follows from the problem.
- Explain why other services are not being recommended yet.

**CTA options:**

- Approve the recommended scope.
- Begin the À La Carte service.
- Begin the appropriate Presence, Momentum, Authority or Growth Partner engagement.

**Guardrail:** Public content must not imply that every prospect receives the same recommendation.

### 7. Renewal, expansion and referral

**Audience state:** The client has received support and is evaluating continued or expanded work.

**Audience needs:**

- Evidence of progress
- Clarity about the next constraint
- Review of what changed
- A reason to continue or expand
- Confidence to refer CMS appropriately

**Primary content pillars:**

- Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning
- Clarity in Practice
- Strategic Social Intelligence

**Strongest formats:**

- Verified Case Study
- Decision-Trace Case
- Process Walkthrough
- Research Interpretation
- Client learning or review content with permission

**Content job:**

- Document learning and outcomes accurately.
- Make the next business decision visible.
- Support renewal, expansion or relevant referrals without manufactured urgency.

**CTA options:**

- Review the next opportunity.
- Continue or expand the engagement.
- Provide a testimonial or referral where appropriate.

## Content-to-funnel mapping matrix

| Funnel stage | Audience realization | Primary content job | Strongest formats | CTA destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recognition | “Something is not working as it should.” | Name the visible problem | POV; diagnostic breakdown; education | Social profile, resource or Social Signal Check |
| Realization | “The visible problem may not be the actual problem.” | Reframe and clarify | Diagnostic breakdown; framework; checklist | Social Signal Check or review request |
| Trust | “CMS understands the situation and has a defensible method.” | Demonstrate judgment and safety | Process; proof of method; case; FAQ | Website method, proof or offer page |
| Intent | “CMS may be the right type of help.” | Reduce risk and clarify entry point | Objection response; decision case; FAQ | Signal-to-Strategy Review or Social Clarity Intensive |
| Diagnostic conversation | “I am ready to examine my situation.” | Prepare and qualify | Process walkthrough; checklist; FAQ | Qualification form, booking or diagnostic |
| Recommendation | “This is the right next level of support.” | Explain scope and fit | Decision trace; case; process | Proposal, contract or service entry |
| Renewal / expansion | “The next decision should follow what we learned.” | Document progress and next need | Verified case; learning; review | Renewal, expansion or referral |

## Content state transitions

CMS content should create a specific next state:

| Current state | Desired next state | Content requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Problem-aware | Name the symptom without overdiagnosing |
| Problem-aware | Constraint-aware | Show possible underlying causes |
| Constraint-aware | Method-aware | Explain how CMS examines the problem |
| Method-aware | Offer-aware | Show the appropriate diagnostic entry point |
| Offer-aware | Conversation-ready | Reduce uncertainty and qualify fit |
| Conversation-ready | Decision-ready | Provide a clear diagnostic process and scope |
| Client | Renewal or referral-ready | Document learning and the next decision |

## CTA governance

A CTA is appropriate when:

- It follows the content's strategic job.
- It asks for a proportionate level of commitment.
- It does not skip necessary understanding or qualification.
- It routes to a defined CMS destination.
- It does not promise unsupported outcomes.
- It is clear whether the action is educational, diagnostic, commercial or relationship-based.

Avoid:

- Asking cold audiences to book a high-commitment consultation immediately.
- Using “DM me” without a defined reason or response path.
- Sending every audience state to the same generic contact page.
- Offering a full strategy as an unqualified free audit.
- Treating likes, follows or downloads as sales readiness.

## Destination architecture

| Destination | Purpose | Appropriate audience state |
|---|---|---|
| Social profile or post | Recognition and continued attention | Unaware or lightly aware |
| Social Signal Check | Low-friction self-identification | Problem-aware |
| Resource or insight page | Deeper understanding | Problem-aware or constraint-aware |
| Signal-to-Strategy Review | Bounded review of a specific situation | Intent-ready |
| Social Clarity Intensive | Paid, finite diagnosis | Diagnosis-ready |
| À La Carte service page | Defined execution or micro-project | Diagnosed and qualified |
| Core service page | Right-sized ongoing support | Post-diagnosis |
| Qualification form | Context capture and routing | Conversation-ready |
| Proposal or client pathway | Formal decision and delivery | Recommendation-ready |

## Evidence and measurement

Measure movement by funnel purpose, not only by reach:

- Recognition; relevant attention, saves, meaningful replies and problem-language signals
- Realization; self-assessment completion, specific responses and diagnostic questions
- Trust; method-page engagement, proof interaction, qualified questions and return visits
- Intent; qualified inquiries, review requests, consultation completion and offer-page engagement
- Diagnostic conversation; show rate, context quality, fit and decision clarity
- Recommendation; scope acceptance, paid diagnostic conversion and follow-on fit
- Renewal or expansion; documented progress, continuation, referrals and appropriate expansion

Do not treat follower growth, impressions or likes as proof of commercial movement by themselves.
