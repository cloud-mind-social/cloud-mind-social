## 3.10 conflict and delay review

| Finding | Resolution now | Remaining risk and next trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 3.8 divided qualified by all submissions; 3.10 divided by reviewed submissions under one name. | Separate “qualified share of all submissions” and “qualification yield among reviewed”; report cohort, cutoff and unreviewed backlog. | Test the human disposition record before the first live cycle. |
| Starts, visits, replies and paid conversion could otherwise use inconsistent populations or implied functionality. | 3.10 specifies deduplication, route eligibility, timestamps, missing-data rules and separate commercial events. | Implement and test events for each promoted route; suppress rates without a measurable denominator. |
| Named owners, response coverage and capacity are only role descriptions. | Responsibility and first-cycle record template are explicit. | Assign humans and backup and verify hours before public invitation or test. |
| External research Green could be mistaken for observed buyer success. | Green is explicitly a planning decision; source-confidence labels are separate from CMS-specific assumption statuses. | Update the Phase 2.5.9 ledger on actual evidence; revisit the gate on contradiction. |
| Client-result proof might be drafted before permission. | 3.6/Phase 4 dual-permission rule carried into handoff and 3.10. | Client-specific use permission and Seul authorization needed before creation or sharing, final use checked again. |

**No unresolved conceptual contradiction blocks Phase 4 strategy planning.** The remaining items can delay *operational launch*: Phase 2.5.6 offer economics and public terms; buyer and trust validation; working destinations and analytics; named owner/backup and response coverage; capacity; and specific proof permissions. Any proposed public CTA that depends on one of them stays planned or awaiting validation until tested.

## Handoff state

Phase 3.1–3.10 are approved planning sources until Seul explicitly reopens an affected decision. Phase 4 can use the shared audience, pillars, formats, CTA types, optional diagnostic routes, governance and measurement definitions. Before a public release or test, use the Phase 3.8 production checks, Phase 3.9 operational readiness checklist and Phase 3.10 first-cycle record, then obtain the applicable separate authorization. Record material learning in Phase 2.5.9; if it affects commercial assumptions, revisit 2.5.10. Phase 5 remains the place for platform-specific strategy.
