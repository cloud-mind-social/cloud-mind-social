## Purpose and boundary

Define what CMS records, how evidence is interpreted, and when observations justify a change to content, CTA routing, destinations or commercial assumptions. Phase 3.8 defines the website conversion events; Phase 3.9 defines operating reviews and role responsibilities. Phase 3.10 connects them into an evidence and decision framework without redefining those events or claiming that any route is live.

Approved Phase 1 and locked Phase 2 decisions remain authoritative. The primary commercial audience is Western growth-stage, owner-led expertise and high-value service businesses that have outgrown DIY or fragmented social marketing. Western early-stage owner-operators are a secondary development audience; Philippine growth-stage businesses are a separate secondary geographic market. Record them separately. Research-supported assumptions may guide planning, but do not become CMS buyer evidence by repetition.

**Documentation approval is not operational readiness or commercial validation.** Numerical baselines, target ranges, buyer response, paid demand, capacity and margin remain unobserved or provisional until recorded directly. Public content follows the [CMS Content Writing Standard](../../CMS-Branding/CMS_Content_Writing_Standard.md), including Grounded human perspective; this internal strategy document uses tables and structured definitions.

## 1. Framework definitions

| Term | CMS working definition |
|---|---|
| Measurement framework | Agreed events, definitions, sources, windows, audience distinctions and owners used to understand movement from content exposure to client outcomes. |
| Validation framework | Rules for deciding whether recorded evidence supports, weakens, contradicts or leaves unresolved a specific assumption. |
| Relevant attention | Attention from someone known or reasonably indicated to fit a defined audience, or able to connect CMS to that audience; general reach is distribution, not buyer fit. |
| Meaningful engagement | A specific question, described situation, request or considered response showing thought about the business problem. A like alone does not qualify. |
| Inquiry | Direct contact or a route submission with enough context for a human response. It is not automatically a qualified lead. |
| Qualified lead | An inquiry reviewed against fit, problem, urgency and readiness with a recorded reason and appropriate next step; not a score inferred solely from clicks or fields. |
| Commercial commitment | An observable decision to purchase a defined scope. Offer, acceptance, contract and payment are separate events. |
| Validated learning | A bounded conclusion supported by recorded observations, with audience, test conditions, counts, limitations and decision stated; not a universal market claim. |

CMS should ask three separate questions in each review: **Did the content reach and help relevant people? Did it produce qualified movement? Can CMS fulfill the resulting offer sustainably?**

## 2. Measurement chain and stage jobs

People may enter at different stages; this is a reporting map, not a compulsory sales sequence.

| Stage | Learning question | Evidence to record | Possible decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Are intended people finding CMS? | Relevant profile visits, identified audience responses, source-tagged visits when available | Adjust distribution, audience emphasis or topic |
| Interest | Do they seek deeper understanding? | Specific questions, resource use, return visits where measurable | Explain the issue more clearly or deeply |
| Trust | Do they understand CMS's method, accountability and limits? | Method and credibility questions, proof interactions, objections, comprehension feedback | Improve method, ownership or proof presentation |
| Intent | Do they connect the issue to their own business? | Specific self-assessment, review request or described business situation | Offer an appropriate route |
| Qualification | Is there a plausible fit and useful next step now? | Fit, problem, urgency, readiness, disposition and reason | Accept, defer, redirect or decline |
| Conversion | Will a qualified prospect commit to right-sized paid work? | Offer made, scope accepted, contract and payment separately | Revisit offer, scope, price or process |
| Delivery | Can CMS fulfill the promise responsibly? | Actual hours, specialist input, revisions, output, cost and client feedback | Adjust delivery or offer economics |
| Proof and expansion | Does verified work warrant continuation, referral or permissioned proof? | Renewal, appropriate expansion, referral, verified outcome and permission | Continue, expand, request permission or learn from loss |

The diagnostic routes are **Social Signal Check**, **Signal-to-Strategy Review** and **Social Clarity Intensive**. They are alternatives chosen according to context and readiness, not mandatory sequential steps. A clearly finite need may be assessed for À La Carte support. Do not count self-assessment completion as a diagnosis or an inquiry as a sale.

## 3. Metric dictionary and recording rules

For every reported rate, store numerator, denominator, observation window and raw count. Identify the event source and responsible role in the test record. Where the event or source cannot be observed, mark it unknown instead of estimating. Segment only when reasonably known and handle private prospect details in the authorized private workspace.

| Measure | Working definition | Primary source and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Relevant audience response | Count of identifiable relevant responses under a recorded audience criterion | Social conversation or referral record; fit may remain uncertain |
| Entry-route traffic | Visits to each diagnostic or service route, with known source | Website analytics if implemented; visits show exploration |
| Assessment completion rate | Completed Social Signal Checks ÷ started checks in the same cohort | Assessment events if implemented; completion does not prove fit |
| Inquiry conversion rate | Submitted inquiries ÷ eligible visits to the relevant route | Route and form events if implemented; do not pool unlike routes |
| Qualification rate | Qualified submissions from a defined submission cohort ÷ all submissions in that cohort; report qualified, reviewed and unreviewed counts and review cutoff | Human qualification record; pending review can depress the rate and must remain visible |
| Time to first human response | Time between submission and first human reply | Intake and response timestamps; automated receipt separate |
| Review attendance rate | Attended reviews ÷ scheduled reviews; record cancellation and rescheduling | Scheduling and attendance record |
| Paid diagnostic conversion | Purchased Social Clarity Intensives ÷ qualified prospects to whom that specific diagnostic was offered | Offer and payment records; interest is not payment |
| Diagnostic delivery | Diagnostics purchased versus delivered with scope and timing | Delivery record; distinguish payment from fulfillment |
| Proposal acceptance rate | Accepted proposals ÷ proposals issued | Proposal record; note revisions and declines |
| Commercial completion | Contracts completed and payments received as distinct event counts | Authorized commercial records; click or verbal interest is not completion |
| Delivery effort and scope | Actual hours, specialist cost and revisions compared with agreed scope and estimate | Delivery and cost records; needed for capacity and margin |
| Continuation | Recorded renewals, appropriate expansions and referrals, each counted separately | Client record; do not attribute all outcomes to one post |
| Loss and friction | Abandonment, no response, deferral and decline with known reason | Intake or review record; unknown reasons stay unknown |

Do not fabricate missing funnel data or calculate a rate with a zero denominator. State the reporting period and any missing or untracked routes. Use simple counts alongside rates, especially with small samples. Baselines come from actual observation before performance targets are set. Targets, if later approved, must include audience, timeframe, capacity and decision purpose.


### Reconciliation before the first test; September 24, 2026

**The resolved 3.8 conflict and owner decision:** Qualification rate has one canonical denominator: **all submissions in a defined submission cohort**. Its numerator is qualified submissions from that same cohort by a stated review cutoff. Report qualified, reviewed and unreviewed counts with the rate. Pending reviews remain in the denominator and may lower the reported rate; do not relabel the reviewed subset as the qualification rate or move pending records to a later cohort. When review coverage is incomplete, describe the result as provisional at the cutoff and report the backlog. Do not compare cohorts with materially different review coverage or qualification criteria as if equivalent.

| Definition or gap | First-cycle rule | Remaining prerequisite |
|---|---|---|
| Cohort and identity | Count unique submissions using a private record ID, route, submitted timestamp and audience when known; duplicate submissions are marked, not silently summed. Fix a reporting window and a review cutoff. | Agree on the actual intake record and data access before a test. |
| Check starts and completions | Record one start and one completion per assessment instance or session in a bounded start cohort; late completions are counted against that cohort at a stated cutoff. | Instrument and test the check if the route is built. |
| Eligible route visit | A route-specific visit with the relevant form or entry action available, using a consistent analytics definition; exclude test traffic when identified. If visit counts cannot be measured, give submission counts without a conversion rate. | Instrument each live route and test form-to-receipt. |
| Human disposition | Record received, reviewed, qualified, deferred, redirected or declined, with reason and review timestamp; “unreviewed” stays visible. Qualified requires recorded fit, problem, urgency and readiness judgment. | Assign an authorized reviewer and cutoff. |
| First response | Start at receipt, stop at first actual human reply; report the response-time distribution only for replied inquiries and separately count pending, missed and outside-coverage cases. An automated receipt is not a human response. | Set feasible response windows, owner and absence coverage. |
| Review and diagnostic purchase | Keep booked, attended, diagnostic offered, offer accepted, contract and payment as distinct dated events; paid diagnostic numerator requires a verified payment for that defined offer. | Agree on commercial record and final offer boundary. |
| Attribution and privacy | Keep known touchpoints and unknown origins; avoid claiming a post caused a sale. Store identifiable prospect and commercial events only in the authorized private workspace; use aggregates here. | Assign access and retention rules to the operational record. |
| Client-result proof | Count permissioned proof only after the client's use-specific permission and Seul's separate authorization before creating or sharing a client-result asset, with final-version use checked. | Both permissions and verified facts for any actual case. |

**Minimum source/role map:** Social or referral response → community or relationship owner; route visit and assessment events → website/analytics owner; inquiry and human disposition → intake/commercial owner; response and review attendance → lead owner; diagnostic offer, acceptance, contract and payment → commercial owner; delivery hours, cost and scope → delivery owner; renewal, referral and proof permissions → client owner. These are responsibilities to assign, not named assignments or functioning instruments. The strategy owner reviews aggregates and the Phase 2.5.9 ledger. A single person may hold several roles once explicitly assigned and covered.

**First-cycle record template:** audience and assumption; route and tested status; start/end and review cutoff; named owners/backups and available hours; exact event sources and privacy location; per-route raw numerators, denominators and unknown counts; observed buyer language; confounders; provisional status; decision and next review date. Create this record with actual values before launching; no baseline or target is supplied by this strategy approval.

## 4. Audience, source and attribution

Keep the primary Western growth-stage ICP, Western early-stage development audience and Philippine secondary market distinguishable in analysis. A connector or referral may be a valuable relationship but is not automatically a primary commercial lead.

Record known source platform, content item, conversation, connector or referral and route when available. Record multiple known touchpoints rather than assigning a sale to a single last click. Use "unknown" when the origin cannot be established. Source association is not proof of causation. Do not compare channels as if effort, audience, distribution and destination readiness were equal.

## 5. Evidence hierarchy and assumption statuses

| Evidence | Permitted conclusion | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Approved strategic decision | CMS has chosen a direction | Does not establish buyer agreement or payment |
| Reviewed external research | Market-informed hypothesis | Does not establish CMS-specific demand |
| Prospect statement | Reported problem, question or intent | Does not establish completed behavior |
| Observed behavior | A specific completed action | Does not fully explain motive |
| Paid commitment | Willingness to pay for that defined scope at that price | Does not validate all offers or markets |
| Delivery evidence | Actual work, effort and bounded outcomes | Does not justify a general guarantee |
| Permissioned client proof | A verified, bounded account of work and result | Cannot imply results beyond the case |

For external research, retain the existing 🟢 High / 🟡 Moderate / 🟠 Anecdotal / 🔴 Unverified source-confidence method; exclude unverified claims from findings. For CMS-specific assumptions, use a separate status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Untested | Reasonable planning basis with no direct CMS observation yet |
| Supported provisionally | Direct observations favor the assumption within stated test conditions |
| Mixed or unresolved | Sparse, conflicting or context-poor signals |
| Weakened | Repeated or consequential observations point away from it |
| Contradicted | Strong direct evidence conflicts; owner reviews the affected decision before it continues as stated |

No automatic numeric threshold changes a status. The owner records the observation, sample, quality, alternatives and consequence. A meaningful contradiction may warrant immediate review even in a small sample; weak volume metrics alone do not establish support. Carry changes into the Phase 2.5.9 assumptions ledger and revisit the Phase 2.5.10 gate when commercial implications are material.

## 6. First controlled validation cycle

Use the relationship-led acquisition hypothesis in Phase 2.5.8 and approved diagnostic architecture in Phase 2.5.5. Actual prospect list, dates, outreach, staffing, capture routes and public invitations require their own readiness and authorization. Before any cycle runs, record:

1. Assumption and audience segment; distinguish primary buyers from development and connector relationships.
2. Problem language, content or conversation approach and intended next action.
3. Specific route and its tested availability; use an approved working alternative if a planned route is unavailable.
4. Owner for inquiries, human follow-up and evidence review; capacity and absence coverage.
5. Bounded observation window and effort; counts and source data that can actually be collected.
6. What evidence would favor, weaken or contradict the assumption; foreseeable confounders and decision date.

Initial questions: Do primary prospects recognize the fragmented-effort problem? Can they distinguish a preliminary check, bounded review and paid diagnosis? What prompts a specific request? Do qualified prospects accept an actual scoped offer and price? Can CMS respond and deliver at viable effort and margin? Which trust objections or broken destinations block progress?

Do not invent a sample-size target or success percentage without a baseline and capacity basis. Record the achieved counts and limits. Do not present roleplay or proxy feedback as live buyer behavior.

### Minimum test and decision record

`Assumption → audience → route/content → intended action → window/owner → observed counts and buyer language → limitations/alternative explanations → status → continue, revise, pause or expand → next review date.`

A public strategy document may hold aggregate learning and decisions; identifiable prospect, commercial and client records belong in the authorized private workspace.

## 7. Review rhythm and decision rules

| Rhythm | Review | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing or inquiry days, when active | Published accuracy, working links, questions, submissions and handoffs | Correction, response, route or hold |
| Weekly, during a test | Inquiry quality, qualification, follow-up, audience language and workload | Next bounded adjustment with owner |
| Monthly or at test end | Stage movement, commercial outcomes, delivery effort, objections and evidence gaps | Continue, revise, pause or expand with rationale |

Each review distinguishes **observed facts**, **interpretation** and **unknowns** and names an owner and next review date.

- **Continue:** Relevant audience understands the message, takes proportionate next steps and CMS can handle the resulting work; retain limits of evidence.
- **Revise:** Relevant interest exists but recurring confusion, poor qualification, abandonment or objections point to message, scope, trust or destination friction.
- **Pause:** A route is broken or unowned, a claim lacks required evidence, a permission issue arises, or delivery cannot support the promise.
- **Expand:** Qualified demand, follow-up, capacity and offer economics all have supporting evidence in the recorded scope. Reach alone is insufficient.

Do not discard an assumption after one weak post without checking distribution, clarity, destination and sample; do not declare it validated after a single positive interaction. Changes to locked decisions still require explicit owner approval.
