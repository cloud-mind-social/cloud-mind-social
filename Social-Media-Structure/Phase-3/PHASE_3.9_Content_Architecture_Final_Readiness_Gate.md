# Phase 3.9 — Content Architecture Final Readiness Gate

**Status:** In Progress  
**Phase:** 3 — Content Architecture  
**Dependency:** Phase 3.8 Content Architecture Operationalization and Production Handoff

## Purpose

Determine whether CMS's Phase 3 content architecture is coherent, usable and safe to hand into Phase 4 — Funnel Content Strategy.

This is the final Phase 3 control document. It does not create new pillars, formats, CTAs, calendars or platform strategies.

## Final architecture audit

### Strategic integrity

- [ ] The content system reflects diagnosis before prescription.
- [ ] Business context remains more important than content volume.
- [ ] Strategic Social Intelligence remains the intellectual territory.
- [ ] The approved Primary ICP remains unchanged.
- [ ] Western commercial priority remains intact.
- [ ] Secondary audiences do not displace the commercial beachhead.
- [ ] Content does not make Phase 2.5 assumptions appear validated.

### Pillar integrity

- [ ] Each pillar has a distinct strategic job.
- [ ] Each pillar has defined themes and angles.
- [ ] Pillars support different audience realizations.
- [ ] Pillars do not collapse into generic social media advice.
- [ ] Evidence, Proof and What We’re Learning remains distinct from generic case-study content.
- [ ] Excluded content categories are understood.

### Format integrity

- [ ] Formats are selected according to strategic purpose.
- [ ] Format roles are distinct.
- [ ] Proof-of-method content is separated from client proof.
- [ ] Verified case studies require evidence and permission.
- [ ] Platform adaptation remains separate from content architecture.

### Funnel integrity

- [ ] Each funnel stage has a defined audience state.
- [ ] Each stage has a clear content job.
- [ ] Content creates a useful next state.
- [ ] CTAs match readiness.
- [ ] Destinations continue the message from the content.
- [ ] Diagnostic and service transitions are proportionate.
- [ ] Renewal, expansion and referral are based on evidence rather than pressure.

### Governance integrity

- [ ] Evidence classes are usable.
- [ ] Claim limitations are recorded.
- [ ] High-risk claims have a review path.
- [ ] Permission and attribution requirements are understood.
- [ ] Hypotheses are labeled.
- [ ] Unverified claims are excluded.
- [ ] Learning can update the assumption register.

### Operational integrity

- [ ] The minimum content brief is usable.
- [ ] Production statuses are understood.
- [ ] Approval gates have an owner.
- [ ] Content learning has an owner.
- [ ] Repurposing rules are documented.
- [ ] Content can be handed to another collaborator without oral explanation.

## Critical unresolved risks

Record each unresolved risk before entering Phase 4:

| Risk | Impact | Current control | Owner | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Limited direct buyer evidence | Content may overstate audience certainty | Use explicit assumption labels and feedback capture | CMS strategy owner | Proceed with assumptions or pause |
| Limited client-result proof | Trust content may rely heavily on proof of method | Separate method proof from client proof | CMS owner | Build proof assets |
| Offer and pricing still evolving | CTA destinations may change | Keep public claims bounded and reference Phase 2.5 | Commercial owner | Stage destinations |
| Platform execution not yet formalized | Content may be adapted inconsistently | Reserve platform rules for Phase 5 | Platform owner | Proceed to Phase 4 only |
| Website or diagnostic destination not ready | CTAs may create dead ends | Mark destination as pending or use controlled manual routing | Commercial owner | Build before launch |

## Phase 4 entry conditions

Phase 4 may begin when:

- The Phase 3 architecture is internally coherent.
- The owner accepts the pillar and theme system.
- Funnel roles and CTA routes are usable.
- Proof and claim controls are operational.
- Unresolved commercial assumptions are visible.
- Any missing destinations have an intentional workaround or build plan.
- Phase 4's scope is limited to funnel content strategy.

## Phase 4 restrictions

Phase 4 must not:

- Reopen the Primary ICP without explicit approval.
- Invent new audience evidence.
- Create unverified proof.
- Finalize platform-specific rules.
- Override Phase 2.5 pricing or offer boundaries.
- Treat content performance as proof of market validation.
- Convert every stage into a direct sales sequence.
- Touch www/ or webmail/.

## Phase 3 decision options

### GREEN — Enter Phase 4

Use when:

- Architecture is approved.
- Controls are usable.
- Missing assets do not prevent responsible planning.
- Assumptions are explicit and bounded.

### AMBER — Enter Phase 4 with controlled assumptions

Use when:

- Some proof or destinations remain incomplete.
- The work can proceed using proof of method and qualified language.
- Manual routing or staged destinations are acceptable.
- No unsupported public claim is required.

### RED — Hold before Phase 4

Use when:

- Pillars conflict with the locked strategy.
- CTA routes depend on unapproved offers.
- Public proof cannot be substantiated.
- The content system cannot be used consistently.
- Audience or commercial direction has become materially unclear.
