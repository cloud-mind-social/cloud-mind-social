## Validation and operational queue

| Dependency | Accountable role for assignment | Required evidence | Trigger or timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer problem, urgency, language and fit | Strategy/commercial owner | Dated actual prospect statements and behavior, audience and limitations | First bounded acquisition cycle; material contradiction immediately |
| Offer economics and qualification | Commercial/delivery owners | Final scope and price, actual hours and cost, margin/capacity model and buyer commitments | Before public pricing or final offer commitments |
| Trust, evidence and permission | Evidence/client owner plus Seul for authorization | Verified claims; client use-specific permission and Seul's separate permission for client-result material | Before creation or sharing of any client-result asset |
| Routes, intake and coverage | Website/intake owners | Tested route, receipt, human response, named backup and realistic window | Before a public CTA or activated channel |
| First-cycle measurement | Measurement/strategy owner | Event capture, raw counts, cohort/cutoff and evidence/decision record from 3.10 | Before live test and at the bounded review date |

**Correction loop:** Record actual observations and uncertainty in the Phase 2.5.9 assumptions ledger; revisit the Phase 2.5.10 gate after meaningful commercial evidence or contradiction; request Seul's explicit reopening before revising a locked or approved strategy decision. Retain Green only for the present planning scope until operational checks are separately passed.
