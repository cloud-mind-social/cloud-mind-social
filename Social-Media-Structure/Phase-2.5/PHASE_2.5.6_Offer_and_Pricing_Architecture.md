## Purpose

Translate CMS's approved diagnosis-first positioning and Phase 2.5.5 lead ladder into a commercially usable offer structure.

This phase clarifies:

- what may be sold as À La Carte;
- what belongs in Presence, Momentum, Authority, and Growth Partner;
- how the Social Signal Check, Signal-to-Strategy Review, and Social Clarity Intensive connect to paid work;
- how the existing CMS pricing research informs directional ranges;
- which boundaries, figures, and assumptions still require validation.

## Important update note

This document is intentionally updateable. The À La Carte menu, service inclusion, scope, packaging, price ranges, and qualification rules may change once figures are finalized through actual quotes, paid pilots, delivery data, capacity review, and willingness-to-pay evidence.

The figures below are directional working bands based on the CMS repository's Pricing Rate Research and Target Market Research. They are not yet approved client-facing prices.

## Governing principles

1. **Diagnosis before prescription:** understand the business problem before assigning a service.
2. **Business before content:** content is an execution layer, not the product by itself.
3. **Right-sized help:** match depth to problem complexity.
4. **Finite entry services:** À La Carte lets a prospect try CMS without creating an undefined retainer.
5. **Outcome-based higher tiers:** deeper engagements are quoted around problem, value, and investment.
6. **Western first; Philippines second:** maintain separate commercial reference cards.
7. **No race to the bottom:** do not compete on cheap posts, volume, vanity metrics, or unsupported outcomes.
8. **No unpaid incubation:** early-stage support must have a defined scope, boundary, and commercial path.

## Offer architecture

CMS should use five layers:

1. **Free recognition asset:** Social Signal Check.
2. **Bounded conversation:** Signal-to-Strategy Review.
3. **Paid diagnostic:** Social Clarity Intensive.
4. **À La Carte execution, operations, profile, and limited strategic services:** finite scope with a defined stop point.
5. **Core engagements:** Presence → Momentum → Authority → Growth Partner.

A hot lead can enter directly at the Social Clarity Intensive. A prospect should not be forced through every stage.

## À La Carte definition

À La Carte means:

> Specific problem → specific deliverable → specific outcome → finite engagement.

It may be priced per asset, per batch, per week, per campaign, or as a one-time project. It is not automatically inferior to a retainer; the correct service depth depends on the buyer's problem.

The internal label Fast Food may describe standardized execution. The client-facing label should be À La Carte Services.

## Core CMS service tiers

The existing ladder remains:

**Presence → Momentum → Authority → Growth Partner**

| Tier | Buyer condition | CMS responsibility | Directional Western band | Philippine reference band |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Presence | Needs a credible, organized presence | Defined presence, limited execution, basic direction | $999–$1,500/month | ₱15,000–₱30,000/month |
| Momentum | Needs consistency and reduced owner burden | Management, execution, coordination, and reporting | $1,500–$2,500/month | ₱25,000–₱50,000/month |
| Authority | Needs strategy, positioning, and optimization | Strategy plus execution connected to business goals | $2,500–$4,000/month | ₱45,000–₱75,000/month |
| Growth Partner | Needs external strategic leadership | Ongoing partnership, specialist coordination, and growth systems | $4,000–$8,000+/month | ₱80,000–₱180,000+/month |

The repository's broader Private Chef reference reaches $4,000–$10,000+ in the West and ₱65,000–₱300,000+ locally. Those are reference ceilings, not automatic CMS launch prices.

The tiers are not four versions of a posting package. Each represents a different level of responsibility and business complexity.

## Early-stage development offer

### Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint

**Audience:** Western early-stage owner-operators with a real offer, early traction, or credible direction.

**Directional working band:**

- Western: $500–$1,200 one-time
- Philippines: ₱20,000–₱45,000 one-time

Potential scope:

- Offer and audience clarity
- Role of social media
- Channel priorities
- Initial messaging structure
- Minimum viable content direction
- Basic measurement logic
- What the owner should build internally
- What CMS can support next

This is not open-ended coaching. A real offer, early traction, credible direction, and a defined need are required.

## Pricing rules

### Price by depth, not by output count

A Reel or carousel can be priced as an asset. A strategy or management engagement should be priced around responsibility, complexity, and business value.

### Keep internal and public pricing different

Internal quoting may use detailed rates by slide, duration, revision, and production complexity. Public presentation should use simplified service names, scope boundaries, and ranges.

### Use separate market cards

Western pricing should reflect the Western buyer context, CMS positioning, and expected responsibility. Philippine pricing should be a separate card based on local market conditions and delivery economics. Do not present Philippine labor arbitrage as the value proposition.

### Use scope reduction instead of arbitrary discounting

When a buyer cannot support the full scope, reduce platforms, meetings, revisions, production complexity, or reporting depth. Do not reduce price while keeping the same responsibility.

### Protect the diagnostic

The Social Clarity Intensive should be paid or limited to qualified pilot slots. A diagnostic credit toward a follow-on engagement may be tested later; it is not yet a permanent policy.

### Define commercial boundaries

Every À La Carte service and tier must state:

- what is included;
- what the client supplies;
- number of platforms or assets;
- revision limit;
- turnaround;
- communication channel;
- approval responsibility;
- exclusions;
- payment timing;
- what happens when the scope changes.

## Qualification and routing

| Buyer signal | Recommended route |
|---|---|
| Wants one specific asset with clear inputs | À La Carte content production |
| Wants content uploaded or organized | Scheduling-only or Social Operations Pilot |
| Has a repeated activity problem but unclear cause | Signal-to-Strategy Review |
| Has an active trigger and needs a decision | Social Clarity Intensive |
| Needs ongoing consistency and reduced owner burden | Presence or Momentum |
| Needs positioning, strategy, measurement, and optimization | Authority |
| Needs external marketing leadership and specialist coordination | Growth Partner |
| Early-stage with real offer and defined foundation need | Early-Stage Social Foundation Sprint |
| Wants unlimited coaching or undefined support | Do not accept without re-scoping |

## Unit economics and validation

Before finalizing figures, CMS should track:

- estimated versus actual delivery hours;
- contractor and tool costs;
- coordination and revision load;
- effective hourly rate;
- scope changes;
- diagnostic-to-follow-on conversion;
- retention and renewal signals;
- objections to price and scope;
- which services create repeat demand;
- which services should be removed, bundled, or moved into a deeper tier.

The initial validation should use paid pilots where possible. Positive feedback without payment or delivery evidence is not enough to finalize pricing.
