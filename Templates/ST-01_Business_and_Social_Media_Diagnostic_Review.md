# ST-01 — Business and Social Media Diagnostic Review

**Template ID:** ST-01  
**Template version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Working master — review and approval required before operational use  
**Primary user:** CMS strategist / discovery lead  
**When used:** After initial inquiry and discovery, before a tailored proposal or strategy recommendation  
**Purpose:** Produce a bounded, evidence-aware assessment of the business's current situation, social-media condition, stated problem and likely constraints.  
**Expected output:** Findings, confidence and limitations, prioritized next actions, service-depth fit, and the strategy modules justified by the diagnosis.  
**Approval owner:** Assign for each engagement.

> **Blank master only.** Save completed client-specific records in the authorized private client workspace. Completing this review does not authorize production, publication, paid spend, or public claims.

## Instructions for use

- Keep the client's stated problem distinct from CMS's interpretation and diagnosis.
- Record the agreed assessment scope before reviewing accounts or materials. Do not imply that channels, analytics, competitors or business operations outside scope were audited.
- Separate observable facts, client-reported information, external evidence, interpretation, assumption and unknowns.
- Attach or link evidence with a date wherever practical. Note access limitations and missing data.
- Do not treat follower counts, visible engagement, posting frequency or appearance alone as proof of business performance.
- Do not promise results or recommend a larger service merely because it is available. Prioritize by business relevance, evidence, feasibility and client need.
- Reuse the existing Claim Register, Assumption Register, Voice and Story Intake, Content Idea Seed and Client Approval Record where applicable. Do not create competing controls.
- Use the terms **[Not known]**, **[Not assessed]**, **[Not applicable]**, and **[Needs validation]** where appropriate. Do not fill gaps with invented facts.
- If using this as a short-form Social Signal Check, state the limited scope and frame findings as signals or initial observations—not a comprehensive audit.

### Confidence guidance

- **High:** direct and relevant evidence supports the finding; material limitations are low.
- **Medium:** evidence is relevant but incomplete, indirect, or has plausible alternative explanations.
- **Low:** the finding relies substantially on assumption, a single unverified report, weak signals, or missing data.

Confidence describes the strength of the current basis, not a guarantee that the conclusion is correct.

## 13. Quality gate before handoff

- [ ] Assessment scope and exclusions are explicit.
- [ ] The client's stated problem is distinct from CMS's diagnosis.
- [ ] Findings are linked to sources and dates where possible.
- [ ] Evidence classes, confidence, limitations and unknowns are explicit.
- [ ] Symptoms are not presented as proven root causes without support.
- [ ] Alternative explanations and needed validation are recorded.
- [ ] Priorities reflect client need, feasibility and evidence—not sales opportunity.
- [ ] Recommendations connect to findings and are appropriately sized.
- [ ] Service fit, responsibilities, dependencies and exclusions are clear.
- [ ] Only justified strategy modules are selected.
- [ ] Existing registers and approval controls are reused where applicable.
- [ ] No unsupported claim, guaranteed result or unapproved proof is introduced.
- [ ] Private client data is stored only in the authorized workspace.
- [ ] The exact reviewed version, decision and next step are recorded.
- [ ] Strategy approval is not confused with creative, publication or paid-spend authorization.

## Source alignment

This template adapts the CMS Strategy Template document's **Business and Social Media Diagnostic Review** requirements: current activity, account condition, previous attempts, stated problem, underlying constraints, evidence, confidence and prioritized recommendations. It also follows the CMS Template document's proposed bounded **Social Signal Check** direction where a short-form assessment is explicitly in scope. It is designed to lead into the Main Strategy Template Library without duplicating existing client controls.
