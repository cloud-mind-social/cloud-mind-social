**Version:** 0.1  
**Master status:** Approved reusable master; approved by Seul on October 10, 2026  
**Primary users:** CMS discovery/commercial owner and authorized client decision maker  
**When used:** After enough discovery or diagnostic evidence supports a scoped recommendation  
**Output:** A client-facing recommendation and an exact service schedule ready to reconcile with the Client Service Agreement.

Copy into the private engagement workspace. Replace applicable fields, remove unused options and exclude internal notes before presentation. Keep Section 2 as the recommendation and Sections 3 through 8 as the service specification. If selected, identify the exact scope version incorporated into the agreement; its document hierarchy controls. This template creates no new prices, legal terms or delivery commitments by itself.
