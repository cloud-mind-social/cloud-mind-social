Clicks differ from website sessions; submissions differ from qualified inquiries; bookings differ from meetings held. Keep acceptance, contract and payment separate; a paid diagnostic requires verified payment. Source association does not establish that content caused an outcome.

CMS's qualification rate uses qualified submissions divided by all submissions in the defined cohort. Include pending reviews in the cohort count and label the result provisional with its cutoff and review coverage. Do not substitute reviewed submissions as the denominator or compare cohorts with materially different qualification criteria or review coverage. When response time is reported, separate the first human reply from automated receipts and disclose pending, missed and outside-coverage cases.

## 7. Decisions and optimization actions

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] | [ ] |

Continue where useful understanding/action and fulfillment capacity support the direction. Revise when evidence identifies confusion, weak routing or avoidable friction. Pause affected work when a route is broken, ownership or permission is missing, a claim lacks support or capacity is insufficient. Expand only when relevant demand, response capacity and delivery economics support it; reach alone is insufficient.

Prioritize the decision with the greatest relevance to the client's objective and constraints. Keep proposed work outside scope visibly separate until agreed. Changes to locked strategy decisions require explicit approval by the authorized owner; reviewing performance does not automatically reopen or overwrite them.
