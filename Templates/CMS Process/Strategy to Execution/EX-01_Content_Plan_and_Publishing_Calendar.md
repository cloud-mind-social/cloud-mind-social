### Proposed statuses for a new client calendar

These labels describe different decisions. Keep them in separate fields and use the evidence rules; do not silently replace a client's existing controlled vocabulary.

| Field | Values and meaning |
|---|---|
| Production status | **Not started:** asset work has not begun; **Creating:** writing/design/editing underway; **In review:** draft submitted; **Revision required:** corrections assigned; **Complete:** agreed production output exists, with remaining approval/release conditions tracked separately |
| Review outcome | **Pending:** decision outstanding; **Held:** necessary confirmation/input missing; **Changes requested:** specific corrections required; **Approved for specified stage:** exact version and authorized stage identified in the existing approval record |
| Publishing status | **Not released:** no release-ready confirmation; **Ready to schedule:** applicable release checks passed; **Scheduled:** confirmed scheduler job exists; **Posted:** live publication verified; **Failed:** attempted scheduling/publication failed; **Cancelled:** planned release withdrawn; **Archived:** item retired, with history retained |

Record the reason, owner and next action for held, failed or cancelled items. A finished asset can remain unapproved. A plan approved for production can remain unreleased. Maintain those distinctions in every view.

For CMS's existing October calendar, retain its established Status and Remarks fields unless a change is expressly approved. Its broad production labels do not replace exact-version approvals or delivery/release evidence. This reusable master proposes a fuller structure for new client calendars.

## 7. Release checks and delivery

Before marking Ready to schedule, verify:

- The exact caption and finished assets match the approved version; sources, voice and rights remain valid for this use.
- The correct account, dimensions/export, slide order, cover and platform instructions are confirmed.
- Necessary destination testing, offer facts, account access, payment conditions and publication authorization are complete.
- The scheduler can open/download the purchased files; the response owner and agreed coverage are identified.

For content-only delivery, name the client or assigned scheduler responsible for release. CMS handover does not establish that publication occurred. The package must leave the recipient no unresolved creative decisions; hold affected items instead of delivering unlabelled alternatives or missing links.
