## 4. Copy and creative direction

Central thought; essential information; what can be omitted: [ ]

Approved brand guide/version; logo, fonts, palette and layout references: [ ]  
Visual or motion direction; information hierarchy and appropriate pacing: [ ]  
Source imagery, filming/shot requirements or illustration approach: [ ]  
Accessibility needs; readable text, contrast, captions and useful alternatives: [ ]  
Copy, caption/script and design working-file locations: [ ]  
CTA placement; supporting context; reason for none when applicable: [ ]

Audience copy must follow grounded human perspective and clear consultative language. Use concrete actions, connected natural sentences and the accountable person's voice. Review neighboring pieces for repeated openings, unnecessary brand naming and predictable structures. Slide count must serve the thought; no fixed hook, numbered lesson or compulsory CTA sequence is prescribed. This structured production brief can use tables and lists.

Present-tense wording describes confirmed beliefs or practices. Preserve uncertainty and conditional wording where needed. Generated or stock imagery must not imply genuine premises, products, events or results that it does not represent.
