## Purpose and boundaries
Use CMS's diagnostic method to build a coherent client strategy supported by traceable evidence. The client-facing Master Strategy combines the relevant conclusions; supporting worksheets preserve research, rationale and operating decisions.
The twelve main templates are included. Content calendars, production campaign briefs, performance reports and specialized conditional templates from the proposed wider library remain separate future work; this pack does not claim to deliver them.
All templates are blank masters. Copy selected documents into the restricted engagement workspace, rename for the client and identify the agreement. Never invite a client to complete the shared masters.
The agreement determines purchased services, fees, quantities, revision windows, support and rights. Strategy recommendations outside that scope require a separately confirmed change.

## Recommended working sequence
1. Confirm the signed scope, usable intake, relevant payment condition, access and authorized approver.
2. Start with 02 Diagnostic Review; define the decision and observed constraint.
3. Use 03 Audience and 04 Competitor Benchmark to test relevant assumptions within the contracted research depth.
4. Complete 05 Messaging and 06 Pillars from the client's business, evidence and voice.
5. Align 07 Channels, 08 Journey/CTA and 09 Creative Direction; identify practical limits and necessary permissions.
6. Establish 10 Measurement with real baselines, definitions and a bounded learning plan.
7. Prepare 11 Roadmap; name owners, dependencies, readiness checks and milestones.
8. Assemble 01 Master Strategy with the selected conclusions and controlled worksheet references.
9. Use 12 Presentation/Decision Summary to confirm direction, changes and unresolved conditions.
10. Begin only the expressly authorized next stage; complete the content brief and separate exact-version release checks before publishing.

These are working dependencies, not a mandatory meeting sequence. Research and decisions may be revisited when new evidence changes the diagnosis.

## Scale to the four service depths
### Content & Execution
Use a focused diagnostic or confirmed brief, the relevant creative direction and a finite roadmap where included. Confirm source, audience, voice and permissions. A full research or strategy package is not automatically included in production pricing.

### Managed Presence
Use a practical strategy summary with a current baseline, client pillars, channel roles, journey/CTA decisions, creative guidance, measurement and ongoing operating ownership. Deeper positioning or competitor research is conditional on scope.

### Strategy & Growth
Use the relevant diagnosis, audience and market work, messaging, architecture, measurement and implementation recommendations. Production, publishing, technical setup and ongoing management are separately selected responsibilities.

### Strategic Partnership
Use the relevant full system with ongoing prioritization, stakeholder decisions, testing, dependencies and review cadence. A retainer does not imply every channel, unlimited tasks or constant availability.

## Shared evidence and approval rules
- Record exact source locations, dates, relevant samples and limitations.
- Separate observed behavior, reported statements, market research, interpretation and hypotheses.
- Assess source confidence from relevance, method, recency and corroboration; a direct source is not automatically conclusive.
- Keep source quality separate from an assumption's status: untested, provisionally supported, mixed, weakened or contradicted.
- Carry unknowns openly; do not create scores, buyer language, proof or baselines without evidence.
- Client brand, audience, offer, objectives, quantities, palette and cadence are client-specific. CMS's own choices are not their default.
- Use the existing private claim, assumption, approval and delivery records; link to controlled versions rather than competing copies.
- A strategy approval is separate from production authorization, publication permission and permission for CMS portfolio use.
- Client-result marketing material requires use-specific client permission and separate CMS authorization before drafting.
- Keep passwords, recovery codes, payment details and unnecessary customer data out of ordinary documents; use the agreed secure route.

## From planning to implementation
A plan must identify whether a destination is planned, exists but untested, working and tested, failed or not applicable. Record the authorized tester, method and evidence; writing a route into a strategy does not establish that it works.
Before release, confirm the complete source/voice brief, rights, exact approved creative version, destination acceptance, payment conditions, scheduler and response coverage. Follow the purchased scope for delivery formats, editable files and support.
For a strategy-only engagement, the client or assigned implementer owns the specified implementation gates. The roadmap documents the handoff rather than implying CMS has executed it.
