## 5. Content expression rules
Audience-facing writing should sound like an accountable person sharing a grounded observation, experience or belief. Retain natural progression, concrete relevance and varied expression.
Use lists and frameworks for strategy documents and functional utility assets when useful. Do not force audience content into repeated sentence patterns, a fixed carousel sequence or a compulsory closing CTA.
