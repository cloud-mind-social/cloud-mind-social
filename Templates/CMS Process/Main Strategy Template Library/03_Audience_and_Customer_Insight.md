## Internal completion check
- Important audience conclusions have an evidence trail.
- Customer language is real and traceable.
- One viral post or isolated comment does not establish an audience pattern.
- Unverified emotional or buying claims remain hypotheses.
- Identifiable customer records remain in the private engagement workspace.
