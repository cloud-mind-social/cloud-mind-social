# Cloud Mind Social ; Change Request and Scope Adjustment

**Version:** 0.1  
**Master status:** Approved reusable master; approved by Seul on October 10, 2026  
**Primary users:** CMS delivery/commercial owner and authorized client approver  
**When used:** A request may change approved deliverables, direction, volume, responsibilities, effort, timing or fees  
**Output:** A traceable before/after decision and controlled implementation instructions.

Copy into the private engagement workspace. One record covers one coherent change; link related requests. The executed agreement controls commercial acceptance and amendments. This record does not itself authorize extra work, additional charges or release.

## Internal completion check

- Classification protects included corrections and distinguishes actual extra work.
- Before/after scope, price, timing and dependencies are explicit.
- Both authorized parties accepted the identified change and required amendments are complete.
- Changed versions receive their necessary reviews.
- All affected operating records reconcile without erased history.
