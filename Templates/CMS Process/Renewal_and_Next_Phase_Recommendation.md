## Internal completion check

- Recommendation follows the next business need and bounded evidence.
- Current work, corrections and finances are reconciled separately from new scope.
- Capacity, deliverables, exclusions, dates and commercial approval support the next phase.
- Coverage gaps and live commitments have explicit owners.
- Renewal and public-proof permissions are not inferred.
