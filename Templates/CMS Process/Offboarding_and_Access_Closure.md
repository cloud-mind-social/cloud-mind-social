# Cloud Mind Social ; Offboarding and Access Closure

**Version:** 0.1  
**Master status:** Approved reusable master; approved by Seul on October 10, 2026  
**Primary users:** CMS delivery owner, client account/workspace owner and receiving operator  
**When used:** A finite assignment completes, a service period expires or an engagement ends through its agreed process  
**Output:** A reconciled delivery, live-work, information and access record with clear remaining responsibilities.

Copy into the private engagement workspace. The executed agreement governs notice, termination, settlement, rights, retention and support. This record documents those arrangements; it does not change them or authorize deletion, messages, campaign changes or removal of account roles.
