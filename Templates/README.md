# CMS Templates

This folder contains reusable blank masters, operating instructions, and the index to CMS's template library. Keep completed client-specific records, account data, research and approvals in the authorized private client workspace.

## Main strategy template library

- [CMS Main Strategy Template Library](./CMS_Main_Strategy_Template_Library.md) — master architecture, status, build sequence, service-depth mapping and governance.
- [ST-01 — Business and Social Media Diagnostic Review](./ST-01_Business_and_Social_Media_Diagnostic_Review.md) — first working strategy template; review and approval required before operational use.

## Existing content development templates

- [Content Idea Seed](./Content_Idea_Seed.md) — blank intake for source material, audience purpose, evidence, claim limits, permissions and separate production/publication authorization.
- [Voice and Story Intake](./Voice_and_Story_Intake.md) — blank intake for natural language, experiences, sensitive boundaries and permission records.

## Library guardrails

- Blank public templates only; completed client records stay in the authorized private workspace.
- Templates must state their user, point in the client journey, required inputs, expected outputs and approval owner.
- Reuse existing Claim Register, Assumption Register, Client Approval Record and other authorized controls rather than creating competing systems.
- Client strategies use the client's approved audience, brand identity, voice and scope. CMS's own brand decisions are not client defaults.
- A completed template or approved strategy does not itself authorize production, publication, paid spend or public claims.

## Build status

The Main Strategy Template Library describes the planned module architecture. A module is **planned** until its own blank master is created, and a created master remains a **working template** until reviewed and approved.
