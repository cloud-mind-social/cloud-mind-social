## Library guardrails

- Reusable blank masters only; completed client records stay in the authorized private workspace.
- Templates state their user, point in the client journey, required inputs, expected outputs and approval owner.
- Reuse existing Claim Register, Assumption Register, Client Approval Record and other authorized controls rather than creating competing systems.
- Client strategies use the client's approved audience, brand identity, voice and scope. CMS's own brand decisions are not client defaults.
- A completed template or approved strategy does not itself authorize production, publication, paid spend or public claims.
